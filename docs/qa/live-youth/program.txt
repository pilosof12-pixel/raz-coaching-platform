Here is your four-week program designed to help you achieve your first Bar Muscle-up and a Freestanding Handstand, while building a strong push/pull foundation and maintaining lower-body athleticism.

This program prioritizes skill quality over fatigue, especially given your age and goals. We'll focus on consistent, high-quality attempts for your Bar Muscle-up transitions and Handstand kick-ups. For strength work, we'll use double progression where you aim to hit the top of the rep range before increasing difficulty (e.g., adding tempo, pauses, or moving to a harder variation).

Weeks 2-4 will build on the foundation established in Week 1. For Bar Muscle-up Transition Drills, we'll aim for slightly more successful reps or less assistance. Handstand practice will focus on increasing successful kick-up attempts and brief balance time. Strength exercises will progress by adding reps, sets, or increasing the challenge through tempo or pauses, following a double progression model.

Remember to listen to your body. If you experience any pain, stop the exercise and try a different variation or reduce the intensity. If you're feeling particularly fatigued from other activities, prioritize the skill work and reduce the volume or intensity of the strength exercises. The goal is consistent, high-quality practice, not pushing through discomfort or poor form.
