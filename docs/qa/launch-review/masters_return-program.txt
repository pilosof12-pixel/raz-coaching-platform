Nine months out, cleared, and three months without leg symptoms means this is a performance-stage return, not a rehab block. We’re rebuilding rowing tolerance on purpose: two direct erg exposures from Week 1, low-spinal-fatigue lower-body strength, and enough trunk/tissue work to support the return without burying recovery.

Weeks 2-3 extend Monday easy rowing and lengthen Friday race-pace pieces; Week 3 also buys back a short third erg touch by trimming general pulling. The longest hard rep in this block is 3 minutes, not race-length yet, because the first job is clean tolerance of rowing volume and split exposure; Week 4 keeps moderate load and split quality but cuts non-essential sets so you finish fresher.

If back stiffness changes your stroke shape or is worse than baseline the next morning, first slow the split by 2-3 sec/500 m or cut one interval. On strength work, swap Goblet Squat or Reverse Lunge for Leg Press Machine and keep Barbell Hip Thrust if needed. Deadlift is deliberately not back in this block; hip thrust plus squat/split-squat/leg-press work covers the pattern until these four weeks stay clean. Stop the session for any return of leg symptoms or radiating pain.
