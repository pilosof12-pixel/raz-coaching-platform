This is a developmental 3K block: 3 direct run exposures, 1 direct 20 kg ruck, and 3 real strength days spread across five calendar days so lower-leg stress and pull-up priority work are better distributed while Back Squat and Deadlift stay on minimum effective maintenance.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the key reps from 400 m toward 800 m while holding pace near your current repeat ability, and the ruck reaches a controlled 10 km in Week 3 with load fixed at 20 kg. Week 4 is consolidation: less nonessential volume, a smaller slightly quicker interval dose, and a shorter Monday easy run kept on purpose to spread impact without dropping your third weekly run.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of Tuesday, repeat the last symptom-free interval week or cut 1-2 reps before changing the easy runs. If it shows up during or after the ruck, keep 20 kg and first slow the ruck pace back to the last symptom-free week before shortening distance. On pull-ups, stop the set when rep height or body control changes; on squat/deadlift, keep the last clean load if bracing or bar path slips.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 13.2 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.

On Improve strict pull-ups from 14 toward 18-20: this block holds it at a maintenance dose rather than developing it, on purpose. Improve 3 km from 13:30 to sub-12:00 and Improve 10 km ruck with 20 kg from 95 min toward 82 min are what this block develops. Keeping the exposure protects what you already have and keeps it ready to progress in the next block; it is not an attempt to improve it in these four weeks.
