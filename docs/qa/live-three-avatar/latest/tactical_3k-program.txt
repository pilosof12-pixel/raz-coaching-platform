Here’s your corrected 4-week block: three real strength days on Mon/Wed/Fri across a five-day training week, with the 3K still driving the week, one direct 20 kg ruck, and separated pull-up priority exposures.

Weeks 2-4: the interval run progresses one main lever at a time: Week 2 adds one rep, Week 3 keeps volume and nudges pace, Week 4 drops volume and lets you express faster repeat speed. The ruck progresses by distance first, then a small pace push with reduced distance in Week 4. Strength stays in a maintain-to-small-gain lane around the running priority.

If shin irritation starts to reappear, first shorten the Friday easy Run by 1-2 km or keep it to 25-35 minutes very easy by feel. On the ruck, keep the Backpack Carry at 20 kg and ease pace before changing load. If needed, trim the Friday Machine Hamstring Curl first; it is the easiest accessory to remove.
