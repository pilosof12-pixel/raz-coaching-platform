This block is developmental for the 3K: we keep your squat and deadlift on a true maintenance dose, preserve three direct runs and one direct 20 kg ruck each week, and put the main progression into the Tuesday interval session plus the Friday ruck distance. The three formal strength sessions are spread across the week so the key run keeps better readiness.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the 3K reps from 400 m toward 600 m at your current repeatable pace while the ruck extends to 10 km with the same 20 kg load and similar brisk-walk pace. Week 4 is a consolidation week: less accessory and interval volume, but enough speed, pull-up quality and barbell load to keep the gains and let you finish fresher.

If shin irritation returns during or within 24 hours of the interval day, first repeat the last symptom-free interval week or cut one rep before touching the easy runs. If it shows up during or after the ruck, keep the load at 20 kg and reduce the newest ruck change first, meaning distance back to the last clean week here; no loaded jogging.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 15.1 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
