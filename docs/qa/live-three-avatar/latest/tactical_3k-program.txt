Short block focus: keep your current 3-run + 1-ruck rhythm, make the Tuesday run more 3K-specific across the month, and keep strength genuinely productive but low enough fatigue that it supports rather than blunts running progress. Squat/deadlift stay in maintenance-to-useful territory, while pulling gets three direct exposures per week without crowding recovery.

Weeks 2-4: progress the smallest useful lever at a time. Intervals move from repeatable 400 m work toward more sustained 600 m reps, then Week 4 trims volume and lets pace sharpen. Ruck work progresses one main variable at a time and stays symptom-gated. On strength lifts, only take the planned load bump if Week 1-3 technique and RPE stay honest.

Pain/substitution guidance: if shin irritation rises above a mild awareness during or the next morning, first hold or reduce the newest running/ruck progression rather than forcing the plan. For the Tuesday run, swap to a flat track/road section and slightly lengthen recovery before increasing pace. If lower-leg symptoms persist, trim Friday easy Run by 10-15 minutes before touching the main Tuesday interval row.
