Here’s your corrected 4-week block built around 3 real strength sessions, 3 direct run exposures, and 1 direct weekly ruck without stacking the strength work on consecutive days. The main build is still the Tuesday 3K interval session and the Thursday ruck pace progression; squat, deadlift, OHP, and pulling support those priorities without turning the week into junk fatigue.

Weeks 2-4: keep earning the top of the rep or pace target cleanly before progressing. Running still moves by one main lever at a time, while weighted pulling and strict pull-up volume stay separated across the week.

If shin soreness returns beyond mild awareness, shorten the easy runs first and hold the current interval or ruck week instead of forcing progression. If the ruck irritates feet, calves, or shins, keep the 20 kg load fixed and back off the pace target slightly until next-day baseline is normal again.
