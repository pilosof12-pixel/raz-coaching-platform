Developmental 3K block: three direct runs each week, one 20 kg ruck, and three real strength sessions. The run quality progresses from repeatable current 400 m pace into longer reps, while squat and deadlift stay on a small maintenance dose so freshness goes to the 3K and ruck goals.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the key reps at similar pace while easy running and main lift doses stay steady; Week 4 trims accessory/easy volume and ruck distance so you can finish fresher and express slightly quicker 400s. This block stays developmental on purpose: the longest fast rep tops out at 800 m while shin tolerance and total workload stay controlled.

If shin pain shows up during or within 24 hours of Tuesday, reduce Tuesday first by stopping at the last clean rep count or repeating the last symptom-free week. If symptoms show up after Friday, hold Friday pace first, then distance, while keeping load at 20 kg. If soreness feels diffuse rather than tied to one session, trim Monday/Saturday easy-run minutes before touching the Tuesday quality work.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 15.5 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
