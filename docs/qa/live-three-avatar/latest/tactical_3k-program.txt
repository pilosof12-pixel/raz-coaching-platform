Keep the easy runs truly easy, and let the Tuesday interval row drive the 3 km progression. Strength stays supportive: enough squat, hinge, press and pulling to maintain strength and move the pull-up goal without stealing freshness from running and rucking.

Weeks 2-4: Tuesday intervals progress one main lever at a time, the ruck progresses with small pace/distance changes only, and the strength work uses simple rep/load progression where recovery stays good. Week 4 trims interval and ruck volume slightly so you can express a bit more speed without stacking fatigue.

If shin irritation returns beyond mild transient tightness, first cut Tuesday interval volume by 1-2 reps and keep pace honest; keep the two easy runs easy. For the ruck, hold distance steady before chasing faster pace. If any lift feels unusually heavy from run/ruck fatigue, keep the same exercise and reduce load to stay in the listed RPE.
