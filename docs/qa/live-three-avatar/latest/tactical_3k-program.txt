Three strength-support days now sit on Mon, Wed, and Sat so the pull-up work stays separated, the Tuesday 3 km session stays protected, and impact is spread across five training days. Squat and deadlift stay on a flat maintenance dose while the primary 3 km work progresses.

Weeks 2-3 make the key run more race-relevant by moving from repeatable 400 m reps to longer 500 m reps, then adding one rep at the same pace. Week 4 trims fatigue and lets pace sharpen on shorter 400s. Pull-ups keep one weighted day, one main bodyweight day, and one small recovery-contingent Saturday touch; the ruck pace improves through Week 3, then holds.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of the interval day, reduce Tuesday first: stop at the last clean rep count, hold pace, and repeat the last symptom-free week. If it shows up after the ruck, keep 20 kg and back ruck pace off 10-15 s/km before changing the runs. On any lift, sharp pain or obvious form breakdown means stop that movement; on Wednesday cut Deadlift to 1 work set if back or hamstrings feel off, and on Saturday drop the optional Pull-up first.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 15.1 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
