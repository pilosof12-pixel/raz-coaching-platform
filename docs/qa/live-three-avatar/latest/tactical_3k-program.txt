Compact 4-week block: your three strength sessions are placed on non-consecutive days so pull-up priority work stays spaced, Tuesday stays the main 3K-specific run, Thursday stays the direct 20 kg ruck, and both squat and deadlift are touched weekly without adding junk fatigue.

Weeks 2-4: Week 2 adds a small amount of interval or easy-volume stress while holding pace stable. Week 3 nudges interval pace while keeping overall impact controlled. Week 4 trims volume and lets you express a bit more speed and ruck pace with less accumulated fatigue. For pull-ups and lifts, keep clean reps first and add load only when the top end is earned.

If shin discomfort returns beyond mild awareness, keep the warm-up, cut Tuesday’s interval rep count first, and hold Friday’s run plus Thursday’s ruck at the last symptom-free week. If lower-leg soreness is worse the next morning, keep ruck pace conservative and avoid pushing the easy runs. For upper-body irritation, keep Pull-up work 1-2 reps further from failure that week rather than forcing progression.
