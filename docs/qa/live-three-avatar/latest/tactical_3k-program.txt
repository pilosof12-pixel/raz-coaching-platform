Here’s a five-day block built around the 3K first: 3 direct runs, 1 weekly 20 kg ruck, and 3 real strength sessions. Squat/deadlift stay on a low-fatigue maintenance dose, pull-ups get one weighted and one bodyweight priority exposure, and Tuesday’s run is the main progression anchor.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the interval reps at roughly your current repeatable 3K-interval speed, while the ruck only gets faster at the same 20 kg and 8 km through Week 3 and the strict pull-up work adds reps. Week 4 trims interval volume for a controlled faster 400 m expression and keeps the best earned pull-up/strength standard without chasing extra fatigue.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of Tuesday, first repeat the last symptom-free interval week or cut 1-2 reps before touching the easy runs. If it shows up after Thursday Backpack Carry, keep 20 kg and back off the pace target first; only reduce distance next if needed. Keep Friday’s easy run flat/soft when possible, and no loaded running.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 15.5 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
