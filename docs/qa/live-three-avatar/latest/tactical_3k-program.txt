Here’s your 4-week block. The week is split across five training days so the 3K session stays protected, the ruck stays direct, and the three gym days remain real strength sessions. The easy runs are written so you can do them later the same day if that keeps the lift crisp and inside the 60-minute cap.

Weeks 2-4 lengthen the key 3K reps from 400 m to 500 m to 600 m, then reduce volume in Week 4 so you can express a slightly quicker 400 m pace. Friday deadlift stays fixed as maintenance, Friday Pull-up volume only climbs if reps stay clean, and the 20 kg Backpack Carry keeps the same 60-minute cap so you gradually cover more ground without changing load.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of Wednesday’s Run, first repeat the last symptom-free interval week or drop the last rep before touching the easy runs. If it shows up during or after Saturday’s Backpack Carry, slow the ruck pace first and keep load fixed at 20 kg. If Reverse Lunge annoys knee or hip, swap it to Leg Press for the same sets, reps and RPE.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 16.3 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
