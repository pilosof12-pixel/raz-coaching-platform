Weeks 2-4: only move up when the prior week’s paces and lifts stayed within the target effort and your shins feel normal the next morning. Week 2 adds interval work, Week 3 nudges interval pace, and Week 4 trims volume so you can express faster 3K-specific speed without stacking the hardest pace onto the biggest workload.

If shin irritation shows up, first reduce the easy run by 10-15 minutes or hold the ruck at the prior week’s distance before changing the main lifts. If needed, keep the interval pace the same and cut one rep rather than forcing progression.
