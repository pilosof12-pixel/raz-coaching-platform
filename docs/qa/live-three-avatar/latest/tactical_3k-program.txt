Keep the gym work tight so the 3K session, three weekly runs, and weekly 20 kg ruck stay the priority. Monday, Wednesday, and Friday are the three real strength days; Tuesday is now your dedicated 3K interval day and Thursday stays your direct ruck day so impact and pull-up stress are spaced better.

Weeks 2-4: the 3K session progresses by one main lever at a time, the ruck progresses mostly by distance before a lighter-volume quicker Week 4, and pulling strength progresses by reps before load. If bar speed, pace control, or recovery clearly slip, hold the prior week instead of forcing the jump.

If shin soreness rises above mild nuisance, keep run frequency but first trim interval volume or Friday run duration before touching squat/deadlift work. For any lower-leg flare, keep the ruck as controlled walking only, avoid pushing the pace, and swap Friday Run for Bike at easy conversational effort until next-day baseline is normal again.
