Here’s your 4-week block built to keep the 3K first, progress the ruck with one lever at a time, and give pull-ups three quality touches each week without wasting recovery.

Weeks 2-4: only the main progression levers move. For the 3K work that means first adding a little interval volume, then shifting toward longer reps, then expressing a bit more speed with lower volume. For the ruck, load stays at 20 kg while pace improves gradually if shin/foot recovery stays normal.

Shin guidance: if shin discomfort rises above mild during a run or the next-day baseline is worse than normal, hold the interval and ruck prescriptions at the last symptom-free week and trim easy-run duration first before touching the main strength work. If needed, keep the easy runs fully conversational and shorten them rather than forcing pace.
