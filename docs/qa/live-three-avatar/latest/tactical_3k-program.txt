Here’s your corrected five-day flow: Mon squat + weighted pull-up + easy run, Wed 3K intervals, Thu deadlift + OHP + strict pull-ups, Sat 20 kg ruck, Sun low-cost unilateral/row support + easy run. Squat and deadlift stay on maintenance so the Wednesday quality run keeps the best freshness.

Weeks 2-4 progress one main lever at a time: longer reps in Week 2, more total quality in Week 3, then a faster reduced-volume expression in Week 4. Pull-up work builds gradually, while the ruck stays at 20 kg and only the pace band moves when the prior week was symptom-free.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of the Wednesday run, repeat the last clean interval week or cut 1-2 reps there before changing the easy runs. If it shows up during or after the ruck, slow the ruck pace first and keep load/time fixed. If Dip bothers the shoulders, use Overhead Press in that slot; if pronated Pull-up irritates the elbows, first reduce load or reps before temporarily using Chin-up.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 14.1 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
