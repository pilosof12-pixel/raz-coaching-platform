Keep the hard progress centered on the 3K interval run, maintain your current 3-run/1-ruck rhythm, and use the three strength days to support pulling and hold onto squat/deadlift strength without stealing recovery.

Weeks 2-4: running progresses by one main lever at a time, ruck progresses with small pace or distance changes rather than everything at once, and pull-up work progresses across weighted strength, submaximal strict volume, and a lighter support exposure.

If shin irritation starts to return, first keep the warm-up, reduce interval count or ruck pace before adding rest days, and swap the Friday easy Run for Bike only if symptoms change your normal next-day baseline.
