Built around your current tolerated load: 3 direct runs, 1 direct 20 kg ruck, and 3 real strength sessions across 5 calendar days. The main progressions are the Tuesday 3K intervals and Thursday ruck. Strength stays productive but supportive so run quality can move without spiking shin stress.

Weeks 2-4: only take the next step if pace stays repeatable, technique stays clean, and next-day legs feel normal. The block progresses one main lever at a time on the run intervals and ruck, then trims volume in Week 4 for a controlled speed expression.

Pain/substitution guidance: if shin discomfort rises above mild or lingers into the next day, first cut one Tuesday interval rep or trim Friday easy-run duration by 5-10 minutes. Keep the ruck as brisk walking only. If elbows or shoulders get cranky on pull-ups, keep the rep target and reduce added load slightly while staying inside the listed RPE.
