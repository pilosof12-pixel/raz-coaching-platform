This block keeps exactly three real strength days on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, with Tuesday as the protected 3K session and Thursday as the direct 20 kg ruck. You keep your current three-run rhythm, one weekly ruck, and three pull-up exposures without junk fatigue.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the Tuesday quality reps from 400s to 600s to 800s, nudge ruck pace one small step at a time, and build pull-up quality; Week 4 trims fatigue and keeps the best standards for a controlled speed expression.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of Tuesday, first hold or reduce that interval step and repeat the last symptom-free week before cutting easy runs. If soreness follows Thursday’s ruck, keep 20 kg and revert the ruck pace to the last symptom-free band before changing run volume; on any rough week, drop calves, Push-up, or Friday Pull-up support before touching the key run.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 16.2 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
