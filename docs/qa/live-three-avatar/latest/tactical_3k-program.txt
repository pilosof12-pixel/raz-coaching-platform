This block keeps your 3 direct run exposures, 1 weekly 20 kg ruck, and 3 real strength days while holding squat/deadlift in maintenance so the 3K stays first. The interval work starts from your current repeatable 400 m pace and lengthens the reps before a lighter/faster Week 4 expression.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the 3K reps and extend the ruck by distance while pull-up work progresses through reps/load. Week 4 trims pull-up and interval volume, keeps the full 10 km ruck distance, and only sharpens ruck pace if Week 3 was symptom-free.

If shin soreness shows up during or within 24h of the interval day, first repeat the last clean week or cut one interval before touching the easy runs. If it shows up after the ruck, keep the 20 kg load and slow the ruck first; if symptoms persist, shorten distance next. On squat/deadlift days, hold load and drop the last set if readiness is clearly off.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 17.7 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.
