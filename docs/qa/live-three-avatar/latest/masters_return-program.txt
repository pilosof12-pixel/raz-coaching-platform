This block is written for someone about 9 months past their injury, at the performance stage of the return, and it progresses on what the tissue tolerates rather than on the calendar. You’re far enough past the disc injury, cleared, and symptom-free in the leg that this block can be a graded performance return, not a rehab plan. The rowing work leads the week, the lower-body strength stays spine-conscious rather than spine-avoiding, and the main reintroduction is purposeful exposure to the erg itself.

Weeks 2-4: Friday’s race-specific work lengthens from 250 m toward 500 m while pace edges closer to your old 2k split; Weeks 3-4 also buy rowing another touch by replacing some general gym work with a short easy erg. Week 4 is a consolidation week: less accessory volume, same moderate effort, and the longest single race-pace pieces of the block. The longest repeat here is 500 m, so we are still short of race-length continuous work on purpose; the next block can go longer once these splits and your next-day back response stay clean.

If stiffness stays at your usual 2/10 and settles by the next day, keep training as written. If a row or lift brings back leg symptoms, numbness, or pain above 3/10 that is still worse the next day, repeat the last clean week, cut one set from the newest lower-body change, and on that day swap Goblet Squat or Reverse Lunge for Leg Press Machine or Hip Thrust; on the erg, keep the rate low and shorten the Friday intervals rather than pushing through rounded, fatigued strokes. Deadlift is deliberately not back yet in this block; Hip Thrust, Leg Press Machine, Goblet Squat, and Reverse Lunge rebuild the same general capacities first without making loaded lumbar flexion under fatigue the main stressor.
