You’re far enough past the injury, discharged, and three months clear of leg symptoms, so this is a performance-stage return, not rehab. Week 1 rebuilds the exact thing you want back with two direct erg exposures: one easy aerobic row and one small, controlled touch of your old 2k pace, while the gym work rebuilds legs and trunk with low-spinal-fatigue choices.

Morning stiffness that settles with movement is acceptable. If back pain becomes more than mild local stiffness, you lose your brace, or rowing posture starts to fold, slow the split or stop that set, then switch the lower-body work that day to Glute Bridge or Hip Thrust and skip the final accessory. New leg symptoms, numbness, or radiating pain mean stop the rowing/flexion-loaded work for that day and repeat the last clean week rather than pushing on. Deadlift is intentionally not in Week 1; Goblet Squat, Split Squat, Hip Thrust, Leg Press, and the erg rebuild the pattern first, and Deadlift comes back once these stay clean during training and for 24-48 hours after.
