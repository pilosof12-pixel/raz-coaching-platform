This block is written for someone about 9 months past their injury, at the performance stage of the return, and it progresses on what the tissue tolerates rather than on the calendar. You are far enough past the injury and already cleared that this first block should train the return, not circle around it. Week 1 gives you two direct erg exposures: one true easy aerobic row to rebuild tolerance, and one short pace-touch session so the 2k split stays in the plan while the gym work rebuilds leg and trunk strength with low spinal-fatigue choices.

Use the normal warm-up stiffness rule: your usual 1-2/10 stiffness that eases as you move is acceptable. Hold the load, shorten the erg piece, or stop the final set if pain climbs above 3/10, if technique starts folding into loaded lumbar flexion, or if any leg symptoms appear. Deadlift is deliberately out of this first block; goblet squat, leg press, split-stance work and hip thrust cover the rebuilding job first, and the hinge can come back next block once rowing tolerance and next-day response stay clean.
