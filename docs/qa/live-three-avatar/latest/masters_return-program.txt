This block is written for someone about 9 months past their injury, at the performance stage of the return, and it progresses on what the tissue tolerates rather than on the calendar. You’re about nine months out, cleared, and well past the protection phase, so this block trains the return to rowing rather than circling the old injury. We keep two direct erg exposures each week: Monday low-rate aerobic work to hold the base at low recovery cost, and Thursday controlled 2k-pace work in small, deliberate doses. Lower-body and trunk strength build around movements you already tolerate, with low spinal-fatigue choices doing most of the work.

Week 2 only lengthens the Thursday race-pace reps. Week 3 deliberately holds the erg dose and asks for one extra Goblet Squat rep only if Weeks 1-2 were clean, so you can tell what changed. Week 4 is consolidation: fewer support sets, the same easy Monday row, and fewer but slightly sharper Thursday reps so you finish fresher while keeping the best quality you earned.

Local stiffness that settles with movement and is back to baseline the next morning is a hold-and-monitor signal, not an automatic stop. If pain starts to travel, leg symptoms return, or you cannot keep a tall stacked posture on the erg, stop the aggravating set, swap that day’s lower-body work to Barbell Hip Thrust or Leg Press Machine at easy RPE 6-7, and repeat the last clean week instead of progressing.
