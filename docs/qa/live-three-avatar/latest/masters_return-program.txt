Nine months past the disc flare, cleared, and without leg symptoms for three months means this is a performance-stage return, not a rehab block. We’ll row on purpose from Week 1, but build the exact movement back with low-rate easy work, short race-pace touches, and spine-sparing strength that supports rowing instead of competing with it.

Week 2 only nudges the Thursday split faster. Week 3 keeps that faster split but makes the work reps longer and buys rowing a third short easy touch on Wednesday by trimming general work. Week 4 is a consolidation week: same moderate loads and best-earned split, but about a third less support volume so you finish fresher.

If stiffness warms out and you are back to baseline the next morning, stay on plan. If back pain goes above 3/10, any leg symptom returns, or erg posture starts folding under fatigue, keep the same load or split next week, cut one set that day, and shorten the row or reduce squat/lunge depth to the last clean braced range.
