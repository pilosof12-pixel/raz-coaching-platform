This block is written for someone about 9 months past their injury, at the performance stage of the return, and it progresses on what the tissue tolerates rather than on the calendar. You’re far enough past the injury, cleared, and symptom-stable enough that this block can train rowing again rather than orbit around rehab. The main target here is rebuilding rowing-specific tolerance and 2 k pace exposure while bringing lower-body and trunk strength back with low-spinal-fatigue choices you already tolerate.

Weeks 2-4 are not four copied weeks: Week 2 deliberately holds the rowing interval dose while lower-body loading steps up a little, Week 3 lengthens the race-pace pieces, and Week 4 trims about a third of the non-essential gym volume while keeping moderate intensity and adding one extra short easy erg touch so more of the week is actual rowing without a fatigue spike.

Morning stiffness that settles as you move is acceptable; radiating pain, leg symptoms, or back discomfort that builds during the session is not. On the erg, if you cannot keep the split within about 2 sec/500 m of target without chasing with the low back, ease the split by 2-5 sec/500 m or stop a rep early. Deadlift is deliberately not in this block: squat, lunge, hip thrust, leg press, and graded rowing are doing the rebuilding first; bring deadlift back next block if these four weeks stay symptom-stable.
