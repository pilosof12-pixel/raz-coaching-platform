You’re nine months out, cleared, and far enough past the injury that this is a performance-stage return, not a rehab block. The plan builds rowing back on purpose: easy specific volume first, one race-pace touch every week, and lower-body/trunk strength from patterns you already tolerate well.

Weeks 2-3 grow rowing tolerance first: Monday easy volume extends, Friday reps lengthen from 250 m toward 500 m, and Week 3 buys a short third erg touch by trimming accessory pulling rather than adding total fatigue. Week 4 consolidates with the same earned standards but less non-essential volume so you finish fresher. The longest race-specific rep here is 500 m; once Weeks 2-3 are clean and next-day stiffness stays at baseline, the next block can extend those reps toward 750-1000 m.

Your usual morning stiffness that settles with movement is fine to train through. If pain rises above 3/10, next-day stiffness is clearly worse than baseline, or any leg symptoms return, hold the previous successful week and first remove the newest rowing or squat/leg-press increase. No deadlift is in this block by design: Goblet Squat, Leg Press Machine, Hip Thrust and Reverse Lunge rebuild the same qualities without asking for loaded lumbar flexion under fatigue.
