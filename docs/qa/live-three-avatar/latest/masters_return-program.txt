This block is written for someone about 9 months past their injury, at the performance stage of the return, and it progresses on what the tissue tolerates rather than on the calendar. Nine months out, cleared, and symptom-free in the leg means this is a training block, not rehab. We’ll rebuild rowing tolerance first, keep one weekly touch near your old 2k split of 2:15/500 m, and use low-spinal-fatigue leg and trunk work to support the return. Deadlift is deliberately left out for this block; hip thrusts, squats, lunges, and leg press cover the strength work while the erg volume comes back up.

Weeks 2-3 add rowing dose first: more easy minutes and longer Friday pieces, with Monday’s 250s only getting quicker if the prior week stayed clean. Week 4 is a consolidation week: less accessory volume and one less 250, while keeping the split and bracing standards you earned. The longest uninterrupted race-specific piece here is 1000 m in Week 3; full race-length continuous work waits until this block and the next-day back response stay consistently normal.

Usual stiffness that warms out is acceptable. If pain goes above 3/10, any leg symptoms return, or next-day baseline is worse, stop the provocative row/lift, keep the spine-neutral options, and repeat the last clean week rather than forcing progression.
