Nine months on, cleared, and with no leg symptoms for three months, this is a performance-stage return rather than a rehab block. You keep two real weekly erg exposures inside the four gym days: Monday rebuilds easy rowing volume, Thursday brings back short contact with 2k pace, and the strength work rebuilds legs and trunk without leaning on loaded lumbar flexion under fatigue.

Weeks 2-3 progress the rowing by lengthening Thursday’s work pieces while keeping them near 2:15-2:17/500 m, and by adding only 2 minutes to Monday’s easy row when the previous week stayed conversational and your back was normal the next day. Week 4 keeps the best earned interval standard but trims about a third of the support volume so you finish fresher, not flatter.

Your usual brief 1-2/10 local stiffness that settles with warm-up is acceptable. If you start chasing the split by rounding, stiffness builds instead of easing, or any leg symptoms, tingling, or numbness return, stop the pace work at the last clean rep, skip the next lower-body row that day, and use Barbell Hip Thrust plus upper-body work only; if Reverse Lunge is the first irritant, swap it for Leg Press Machine at the same target RPE and repeat the last clean week.
