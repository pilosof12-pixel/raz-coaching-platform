This is Weeks 1-4 of your 8-week run-in: a prepeak specificity build block, not a taper. The classic lifts get the best slots and the heaviest progression, front squat gets enough real dose to stay strong, and back squat plus pulling stay on maintenance while you sharpen the competition lifts during the cut.

Weeks 2-3 push the competition-lift load ranges up with the same structure. Week 4 keeps the Tuesday and Wednesday technical work steady and only nudges the heavier Monday, Thursday and Friday singles if Week 3 was clean.

Use your normal wrist prep before every front-rack day. If the left wrist changes from the usual settling discomfort to sharper or lingering pain, stay at the bottom of the load range, cut the last primary set, and use straps on Front Squat that day rather than forcing more rack volume. Flat days while making weight are not a cue to add work; weight-cut strategy itself is outside this plan.
