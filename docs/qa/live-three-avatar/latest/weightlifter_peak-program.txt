Weeks -8 to -5 from the national qualifier: this is a prepeak specificity build, not the taper. Monday, Wednesday, and Friday keep the classic lifts as the priority; Tuesday and Thursday stay low-fatigue support focused so the heavier midweek and Friday work get the best readiness while front squat, back squat, and pulling strength stay in place.

Weeks 2 and 3 only move the classic-lift bar weights up if the prior week stayed inside the RPE cap with clean positions. Week 4 is a consolidation week: nonessential volume drops by about a third, but the main lifts stay moderate-heavy so you finish fresher for the next block.

For the left wrist, keep the band and wrist prep before every Clean and Jerk exposure. If rack discomfort rises above mild awareness, hold the listed load or drop 3-5% that day; if it persists, stop the remaining Clean and Jerk sets, complete the non-provocative work, and repeat the last clean pain-free load next time. If Friday barbell pulling irritates the low back, use Chest-Supported Row at the same sets, rest, and target RPE.
