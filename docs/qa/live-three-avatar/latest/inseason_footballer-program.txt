These two post-football gym sessions are there to keep you selectable and keep your usual speed/strength qualities alive through the season, not to chase soreness or gym PRs. Your field work already gives you most of the week’s sprint, change-of-direction and repeated-effort load, so the gym just tops up three things: a small speed touch on MD-4, squat/hamstring maintenance, and a very small MD-2 repeated-sprint contact.

Weeks 2-3 progress only one lever at a time: Week 2 adds one acceleration rep if Week 1 stayed crisp and the next-day hamstring baseline stayed normal; Week 3 holds that and slightly tightens the shuttle recovery if Week 2 felt controlled. Week 4 is a consolidation match week: main lift loads stay the same, non-essential volume drops, and finishing fresher is the win.

Pain/substitutions: any sharp pain, radiating symptoms, giving-way, or a hamstring grab ends that row immediately. If the right hamstring feels more than mild tightness on Sprint, Shuttle Run or Nordic Hamstring Curl, cut the last rep first; if it still lingers, swap that hamstring row that day to Machine Hamstring Curl at RPE 6-7 and keep the rest of the session short and clean.
