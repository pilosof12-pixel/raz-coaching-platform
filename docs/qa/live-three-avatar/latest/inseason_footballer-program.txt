These two gym sessions sit at MD-4 and MD-2 to keep you selectable and fast, not to chase fatigue. Football already supplies most of the week’s running and repeat-effort load, so the added work here is just one clean straight-sprint touch, one short shuttle touch, minimum-dose squat and hamstring strength, and low-soreness sled work.

Weeks 2-3 only progress the sled volume if the prior week stayed clean; the sprint and shuttle touches stay at the same low dose and progress through cleaner reps with no fade, while the squat, hip thrust, Nordic and upper-body work deliberately hold steady. Week 4 is a consolidation week: same moderate intensity on the main lifts, but less running and accessory volume so you finish fresher without giving anything away.

If the right hamstring feels more than mild tightness, stop Sprint or Shuttle Run first, then Prowler Push, and keep only the clean strength work. If Nordics feel edgy or crampy, swap that day to Machine Hamstring Curl for the same sets and keep 2-3 reps in reserve. Any sharp pain, loss of push-off, or clear asymmetry means end that pattern for the day.
