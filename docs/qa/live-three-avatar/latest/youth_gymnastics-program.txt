Skills come first while you’re fresh: controlled handstand entries and bar muscle-up transition work lead both sessions, then support strength. Keep everything clean and submaximal, stop before misses pile up or speed drops.

Weeks 2-4: the main progressions are cleaner handstand entries with a few more quality attempts, less help / more total clean turnover work on the bar muscle-up drill, and small rep increases on support strength. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard but trims a little volume.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, cut the last support exercise first, then reduce handstand or transition attempts before changing the whole session. If a movement causes sharp pain, stop that exercise that day and use the same pattern with more assistance or less range.
