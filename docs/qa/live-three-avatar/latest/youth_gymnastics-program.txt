Two skill-first sessions each week. Spread Session A and Session B by at least 48 hours when you can, and keep every skill rep clean and repeatable rather than chasing tired attempts.

Weeks 2-3 reduce band help on the bar transition work and raise the kick-up catch and wall-float/toe-pull standard while most support work stays steady; Week 3 adds a small rep step for Pistol Squat and Ring Push-up. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 band level, balance standard, and jump quality, but trims total sets.

If you get sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or your technique visibly changes, stop that row right away. For bar work, go back to the previous band immediately; for handstands, shorten the kick-up and continue only with Handstand Hold that stays stacked; for Ring Dip or Pistol Squat, reduce ROM or pause demand and repeat the last clean week.
