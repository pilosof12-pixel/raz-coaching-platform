Skills stay first while you are fresh. Session A leans bar turnover and explosive pulling; Session B leans handstand balance plus pistols and jumps.

Weeks 2-3 reduce bar-muscle-up assistance and raise the handstand standard from brief 1-second catches to cleaner 2-3-second sticks and wall floats. Week 4 trims attempts and support volume, but keeps the best Week-3 band and balance standard.

Sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or any visible technique change, ends that row for the day. For bar turnover, add band or foot assistance instead of forcing misses; for handstands, come down early and shorten the hold or float count instead of chasing a save.
