Skills stay first while you’re fresh: bar muscle-up practice, then handstand balance work, then support strength. Keep everything clean and repeatable; stop early if speed, shape, or symmetry drops.

Weeks 2-4: the block aims to reduce bar-muscle-up assistance gradually, improve explosive pull height and speed, and build calmer handstand entries with better balance control. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard but trims fatigue.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, shorten the handstand hold time first, then reduce kick-up attempts. If bar transition reps get asymmetric or grindy, add assistance back and keep the reps as clean singles.
