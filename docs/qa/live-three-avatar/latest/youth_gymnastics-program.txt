Two skill-first sessions each week. Session A leans bar muscle-up, Session B leans handstand, and both primary skills are practiced fresh in both sessions. Keep every skill rep clean and stop before quality drops.

Weeks 2-3 reduce bar-muscle-up assistance, build a little more handstand entry/control, and progress ring dips, rows, pistols, and hamstring work conservatively. Week 4 trims some volume but keeps the best Week 3 skill standard.

Sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or any obvious technique change, ends that exercise for the day. On handstands, shorten the hold and keep more wall help; on bar transitions, add more bench or band help; on ring dips, switch that day to Ring Push-up and keep it easy.
