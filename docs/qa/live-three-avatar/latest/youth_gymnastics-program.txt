Skills come first while you’re fresh. Session A leans bar muscle-up, Session B leans handstand plus lower-body athleticism. Keep everything clean and submaximal, stop before misses stack up or speed drops.

Weeks 2-4: the main progression is cleaner bar-muscle-up transition doubles with less help, plus more controlled handstand entries and slightly higher reduced-support handstand volume. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standards but trims fatigue.

If you feel sharp or local joint pain in the wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee, stop that exercise for the day and regress it. For handstands, shorten the set or use more wall support. For bar muscle-up work, use more band help if turnover gets slow or uneven.
