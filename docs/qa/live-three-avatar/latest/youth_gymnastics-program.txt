Skills stay first each session while you’re fresh. Keep every rep and attempt clean, stop before misses pile up, and use just enough band help on bar work to stay fast and symmetrical.

Weeks 2-4: the main progressions are less band help on Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill, a small rise in clean handstand practice with less wall reliance, and a harder lower-body control demand by Week 3. Week 4 keeps the best Week-3 standard with a little less total fatigue.

If you feel sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or your shape changes noticeably, stop that exercise for the day and regress it: use more band help on Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill, reduce kick-up attempts, or swap 5-Second Eccentric Pistol Squat back to Pistol Squat.
