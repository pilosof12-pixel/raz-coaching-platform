Skills come first each session while you’re fresh: bar muscle-up practice and handstand balance first, then support strength. Everything is submaximal and clean, stop early if reps or attempts get sloppy.

Weeks 2-4: the main progressions are less assistance and cleaner turnover on Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill, plus more controlled handstand entries and slightly longer wall-supported holds. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard with a little less total fatigue.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, cut the last support exercise first, then reduce skill attempts before changing the whole session. Stop any skill row after repeated misses or loss of shape.
