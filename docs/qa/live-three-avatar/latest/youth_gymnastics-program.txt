Skills stay first in both sessions: bar muscle-up pattern and freestanding handstand entry work are trained fresh, then support strength. Keep every rep clean, stop early if technique drops, and avoid repeated failed skill attempts.

Weeks 2-4: bar muscle-up work now shows visible TSV progression through a small increase in clean direct-skill volume in Week 2, then clean doubles with lighter assistance in Week 3, with Week 4 consolidating that better standard at lower fatigue. Handstand still progresses through better kick-up control and a little more balance capacity without turning practice into fatigue.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, first cut 1 set from support work, then reduce skill attempts. For handstands, use shorter holds and fewer kick-ups if wrists feel cranky. For bar muscle-up transition work, add a little more band help if the turnover gets slow or uneven.
