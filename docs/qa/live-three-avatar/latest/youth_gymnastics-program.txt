Skills stay first while you’re fresh: each session starts with handstand balance practice and bar muscle-up work, then support strength. Stop every set before form breaks down, and keep failed reps or attempts very low.

Weeks 2-4: handstand work progresses through cleaner entries and more total quality attempts before Week 4 trims fatigue but keeps the Week 3 entry standard. Bar muscle-up work progresses through cleaner turnover reps with less help, while support strength builds steadily without grinders.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, cut the last accessory first, reduce kick-up attempts, and add a bit more band help on Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill. If pistol depth or control gets sloppy, keep the same rep target and slow the lowering instead of forcing more reps.
