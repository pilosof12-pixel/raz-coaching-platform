Skill work comes first each session so your handstand and bar muscle-up practice stay sharp and successful, not tired. Keep every rep clean, stop before technique slips, and avoid repeated failed attempts.

Weeks 2-4: the main progressions are slightly more controlled handstand attempts, longer wall-supported handstand capacity, and less help on the bar muscle-up transition when the turnover stays close, fast, and even. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard with a little less total fatigue.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, first reduce kick-up attempts or use a slightly easier band on the Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill. Stop any rep that becomes asymmetrical, crashes onto the bar, or forces a hard press-out.
