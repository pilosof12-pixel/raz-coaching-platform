Skills go first every session while you’re fresh. Keep every rep and attempt clean, stop before form breaks, and avoid repeated misses.

Weeks 2-4: the plan progresses by cleaner bar transition work with a little less assistance, slightly more controlled handstand attempts, and more control on support strength. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 skill standard but trims fatigue a bit.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, reduce kick-up attempts first, then add a bit more band help on Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill. If pistols irritate the knee, shorten range only as needed and keep the slow lower/pause.
