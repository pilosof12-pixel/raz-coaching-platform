Two focused sessions per week, with bar muscle-up and freestanding handstand practice first while you’re fresh. The goal is clean reps and good attempts, not fatigue or failed grinding.

Weeks 2-4: bar muscle-up work progresses mainly by using less band help and cleaner turnover mechanics; handstand progresses by slightly more high-quality attempts plus longer wall-supported position holds. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard but trims fatigue.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, stop the set when form changes. For handstands, shorten hold time or reduce kick-up attempts before removing the session. For bar muscle-up work, use more band help again if the turnover gets slow, uneven, or painful.
