Skill work stays first in both sessions so the bar muscle-up and freestanding handstand are always trained fresh. Treat every listed skill dose as a ceiling, not a quota: stop early whenever turnover symmetry, bar speed, or handstand line drops.

Weeks 2-3 progress by slightly reducing bar-muscle-up help, tightening the handstand balance standard, and adding small reps to support strength. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 skill standard but trims sets so you can lock it in cleanly.

Any sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or any visible technique change: stop that row. Add assistance on the Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill, use a wall catch on Controlled Handstand Kick-up, and switch to Assisted Pistol Squat or an easier ring angle/height for push and pull work if needed.
