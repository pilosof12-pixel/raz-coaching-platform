Train the two primary skills first, while you’re fresh: clean bar transition practice and controlled handstand entries come before support strength. Everything stays submaximal and repeatable, no grinders, no chasing failed reps.

Weeks 2-4: the main progressions are less band help / cleaner turnover on the bar muscle-up drill, better kick-up control and a few more quality handstand attempts, plus small rep or hold-time increases on support work. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standards but trims a little fatigue.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders feel sharp pain, stop that movement for the day. For handstands, reduce attempts and use a slightly easier wall distance. For bar transition work, add more band help rather than forcing a slow or uneven turnover.
