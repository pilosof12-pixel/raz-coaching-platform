Skills stay first every session. Session A leans bar muscle-up, Session B leans handstand plus lower-body athleticism. Stop every skill set while reps are still clean, no grinders and no repeated failed attempts.

Weeks 2-4: we nudge one clear variable at a time, more clean bar-transition singles, then less assistance, then a little more handstand entry practice. Week 4 trims fatigue but keeps the best Week-3 standards.

Pain guide: normal effort and next-day muscle fatigue are fine. Sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain is not. If handstand work irritates the wrists, shorten the hold and keep only clean kick-ups. If the bar transition gets asymmetrical or painful, add assistance immediately or stop that row and keep the easy Ring Row or Ring Support Hold. If pistols bother the knee, switch that day to Assisted Pistol Squat and stay pain-free.
