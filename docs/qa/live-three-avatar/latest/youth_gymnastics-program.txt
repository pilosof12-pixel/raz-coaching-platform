Skills stay first each session. Stop early any time the bar turnover gets slow or uneven, or your handstand entries start getting worse.

Weeks 2-3 reduce band help on the bar transition drill, add a little more clean handstand practice/support time, and only add strength reps when every set stays crisp. Week 4 keeps your best Week 3 skill standard with a bit less total work.

Normal muscle effort is fine. Sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or any obvious form change, means stop that exercise right away: use more band help on the transition drill, catch kick-ups to the wall sooner, or swap Ring Dip or Ring Push-up to Push-up for the day.
