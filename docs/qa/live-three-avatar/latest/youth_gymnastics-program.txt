Skills stay first while you are fresh. Session A leans bar muscle-up, and Session B leans handstand balance plus lower-body athleticism. Everything is submaximal, clean, and repeatable, no grinders and no chasing misses.

Weeks 2-4: the bar transition drill progresses by using less band help when every rep stays fast and symmetrical. Handstand work progresses by a small rise in fresh attempt volume and longer reduced-support wall holds through Week 3. Week 4 keeps the best Week-3 standards with fewer total sets.

If you get sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, stop that row that day. For handstands, shorten the hold or use a little more wall help. For the bar transition, go back to the last clean band level. For pistols, swap to Assisted Pistol Squat if balance or knee tracking changes.
