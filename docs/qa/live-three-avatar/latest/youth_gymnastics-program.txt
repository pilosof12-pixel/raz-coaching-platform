Skills come first each session while you’re fresh: controlled handstand entries and bar-specific muscle-up practice before the strength work. Stop any skill set early if reps or attempts get messy.

Weeks 2-4: the plan progresses by cleaner kick-up attempts, a little more handstand capacity, and less assistance or more quality on bar muscle-up transition work. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard with a little less fatigue.

Pain/substitutions: no grinding, no repeated failed bar muscle-up turnovers, and stop any wrist/shoulder/elbow discomfort early. If kick-ups irritate wrists, reduce attempts and keep the wall-supported handstand work. If bar transition quality drops, use more band assistance that day rather than forcing it.
