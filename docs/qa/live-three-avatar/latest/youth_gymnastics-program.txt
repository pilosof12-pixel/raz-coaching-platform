Session A keeps the bar muscle-up work first, then handstand entry practice, then support strength. Session B keeps the handstand balance work first, includes lighter bar turnover practice, and keeps the lower-body athletic work in.

Weeks 2-4: Week 2 reduces assistance and adds a little more wall-control time. Week 3 keeps the same clean standards and adds a small amount of earned support volume or cleaner doubles. Week 4 trims fatigue but keeps the best Week-3 skill standard instead of resetting.

If you feel sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, stop that exercise right away. For handstands, go back to a smaller kick-up or more wall contact. For bar turnover work, add more band or bench/toe help. For dips or pistols, shorten the range only enough to stay smooth and pain-free.
