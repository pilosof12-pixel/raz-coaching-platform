Skills come first every session while you’re fresh. The main focus is clean bar-specific muscle-up practice and controlled handstand entry/balance work, then support strength, then lower-body athletic work.

Weeks 2-4: the plan progresses by cleaner kick-ups, slightly less band help or stricter turnover on the bar transition drill, a bit more wall-handstand capacity, and conservative rep progressions on support strength. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standard but trims a little fatigue.

If any wrist, elbow, or shoulder pain shows up, stop that exercise for the day and switch to the easier band-assisted version or shorten the range to pain-free clean reps only. For handstands, if kick-ups get messy, end the set early and use the wall-supported hold quality instead of forcing misses.
