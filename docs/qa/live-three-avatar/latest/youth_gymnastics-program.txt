Skills come first in both sessions while you’re fresh. We’ll practice bar-specific turnover and handstand balance every session, then build support strength with clean, repeatable reps and no repeated misses.

Weeks 2-4: the main progressions are less band help on the bar transition drill, slightly more total high-quality kick-up practice, and small rep/hold increases on the support work. Week 4 trims some volume but keeps the best clean Week 3 standard.

If wrists, elbows, or shoulders get sore, stop the set when balance or turnover quality drops. For any pain, reduce kick-up attempts first, add a bit more band assistance on the transition drill, and keep all pulling/dip work comfortably submaximal.
