Here’s your corrected 5-day park block. Monday keeps the best readiness for the main ring muscle-up plus Weighted Pull-up session, Friday is the lower-cost weighted support day, and the straight-arm work stays split so planche/front lever do not steal elbow recovery. Tuesday keeps freestanding handstand work plus pike strength with real pulling underneath it, and Wednesday power stays to full-reset broad jumps to fit the park setup.

Weeks 2-3 add one Monday ring muscle-up single and 1 kg to Monday Weighted Pull-up only if the prior week stayed inside the RPE cap; Friday Weighted Pull-up, front lever maintenance, and most support work stay steady on purpose. Week 4 is consolidation: same Week 3 standards, fewer total sets, so you finish fresher without losing strength or skill quality.

If the medial elbow ache climbs above your usual 2/10 or lasts past 48 hours, first trim the straight-arm work most likely to have caused it: drop Friday Advanced Tuck Planche first, then Friday Band-Assisted Straddle Planche or Tuesday Advanced Tuck Planche before touching Weighted Pull-up or Dip work. If a Bulgarian Split Squat setup feels unstable at the park, use light fingertip balance support on the bar and keep the effort target rather than forcing load.
