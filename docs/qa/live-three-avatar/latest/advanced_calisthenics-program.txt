Week 1 keeps the main pulling goals in the best slots: Monday is clean Strict Muscle-up singles plus the lighter Weighted Pull-up volume work, and Friday is the set-length Strict Muscle-up ladder plus the heavier Weighted Pull-up strength work. Planche gets two specific exposures, front lever is held at true maintenance, and Saturday stays deliberately low-cost so the week finishes fresher instead of just fuller.

Weeks 2-3 progress the heavy Weighted Pull-up directly: 30 kg doubles become 30 kg triples, then 32 kg doubles if Week 2 stays crisp. Strict Muscle-up progresses through cleaner set length and lower assistance, not extra fatigue. Week 4 is a consolidation week: keep the best standards, trim non-essential volume, and finish fresher.

Elbow guidance: if the usual ache follows straight-arm work, first cut Tue Band-Assisted Straddle Planche, then the Tue/Sat Advanced Tuck Planche rows, then Wed Advanced Tuck Front Lever, and only then touch the Mon/Fri bent-arm pulling rows. If symptoms instead follow bent-arm pulling, trim Sat Ring Push-up first, then Wed Advanced Tuck Front Lever, then Mon Weighted Pull-up 27.5 kg 3x3, and only if it still builds reduce Fri Weighted Pull-up from its heavy row. That order is only the default: if one named row clearly reproduces the ache and an earlier row does not, reduce the provoking row first and leave the asymptomatic one alone. If recovery slips before pain appears, same load feeling harder, worse sleep, slower warm-ups, proactively trim Mon Weighted Pull-up 27.5 kg 3x3 from 3 sets to 2 and keep Friday heavy pulling intact.
