Built around two main bent-arm priority slots: Monday is heavy Weighted Pull-up with crisp Ring Muscle-up singles, and Friday is your set-length Ring Muscle-up day with the Weighted Pull-up volume row. Straight-arm work stays present but budgeted, with the bridge planche exposure only once per week and Saturday kept deliberately lower-cost.

Weeks 2-3 nudge Monday Weighted Pull-up load up, keep Friday as the consecutive-rep driver for Ring Muscle-up, and move handstand/planche mostly through cleaner reps or longer holds rather than a big fatigue jump. Week 4 trims roughly a third of the non-essential volume so you absorb the block and finish fresher without backing away from the main skills.

If the usual straight-arm elbow ache shows up, cut the row that clearly reproduces it first; otherwise use this default order: Tue Band-Assisted Straddle Planche, Sat Advanced Tuck Planche, Tue Advanced Tuck Planche, Mon Advanced Tuck Front Lever, and only then the bent-arm pulling rows. If it instead follows bent-arm pulling, trim Wed Inverted Row first, then Mon Ring Muscle-up quality singles, then Fri Weighted Pull-up volume, and touch Mon Weighted Pull-up heavy only if the first three changes do not settle it. If recovery slips before pain appears, same load feels harder, sleep worsens, warm-ups feel slow, reduce Fri Weighted Pull-up volume first.
