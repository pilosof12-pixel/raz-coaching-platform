Five park sessions, with Mon and Sat owning the two bent-arm priorities, Tue and Fri holding the straight-arm and handstand work apart for the elbow, and Wed kept as the low-cost bridge day. The Mon/Sat Muscle-up rows are your strict ring practice: false grip, clean turnover, and the listed attempts are ceilings, not quotas.

Week 2 nudges load, set length, or hold quality. Week 3 is the hardest week: the top ladder set reaches 3 clean reps and pull-up loading peaks. Week 4 cuts about a third of the non-essential volume so you keep the strength but finish fresher.

If the usual medial-elbow ache follows straight-arm work, first cut Fri Band-Assisted Straddle Planche, then trim Tue and Fri Advanced Tuck Planche, then reduce Fri Advanced Tuck Front Lever; only if it still lingers do you reduce Sat Weighted Pull-up before touching Mon Weighted Pull-up. If soreness instead follows bent-arm pulling, first drop Tue Inverted Row, then Fri Advanced Tuck Front Lever, then Sat Weighted Pull-up and Sat Muscle-up, and only if it persists reduce Mon Weighted Pull-up and Mon Muscle-up. Any pain spike or clear form breakdown means stop that row, repeat the last clean successful week, and keep the better-tolerated work.
