This block keeps the main recovery budget on direct Muscle-up and direct Weighted Pull-up work, with straight-arm volume kept specific but restrained so the elbow does not pay for the secondary goals. Monday and Thursday carry the two pull priorities, Tuesday and Friday build the freestanding handstand push-up pathway, and Wednesday stays the low-cost full-body day.

Weeks 2-4: Week 2 keeps Monday pull-up load steady while Thursday muscle-up work moves from singles to clean doubles and Tuesday handstand negatives add a second set. Week 3 moves Monday Weighted Pull-up to 32 kg doubles and keeps the rest stable. Week 4 is a real consolidation week: fewer support sets, same main-lift intensity, and a fresher finish.

If the left medial elbow ache rises above 3/10 or lasts beyond 48 hours, first remove the Thursday Band-Assisted Straddle Planche row, then trim Advanced Tuck Planche and Advanced Tuck Front Lever holds by 2-3 seconds, and only turn Thursday muscle-up doubles back to singles if the elbow is irritated during the turnover or support. Keep Weighted Pull-up if bent-arm pulling is still symptom-free. On handstand days, stop the set as soon as balance or shoulder line breaks.
