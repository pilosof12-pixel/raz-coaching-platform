This 4-week block is the final runway into the national qualifier: Weeks 1-2 are realization, Week 3 is taper, and Week 4 is competition week. The snatch and clean and jerk stay the priority, front squat is held with just enough dose, and back squat plus pulling strength stay on maintenance so more freshness shows up on the platform.

Week 2 nudges the competition lifts heavier without adding support volume. Week 3 keeps the heavier feel but strips sets, and Week 4 shifts to short countdown touches and a brief Day -1 primer so you arrive sharp, not flat.

Use your normal wrist prep before every front-rack day. If the left wrist goes beyond mild discomfort, keep the same load, cut the last set, and drop extra front-rack volume rather than forcing it; any repeated miss, press-out risk, or obvious bar-speed drop ends that exercise for the day.
