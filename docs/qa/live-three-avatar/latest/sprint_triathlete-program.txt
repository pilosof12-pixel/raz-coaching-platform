Week 1 sits about 8 weeks out from race day, so this is a prepeak specificity build week, not a taper. The week keeps your run frequency exactly at the current tolerated three-run pattern, puts the most precise work into the swim, adds one short race-order brick rehearsal, and keeps strength supportive rather than fatiguing.

Achilles guidance: keep every run flat or on the treadmill, with no hills and no extra running outside the three planned run days. If stiffness is above your normal next-morning baseline, first reduce the session that most likely caused it: Monday means cut the second brick pair or ease the run rep pace slightly; Saturday means trim 1-2 km from the long run next time. Keep Wednesday easy unless soreness feels diffuse from the whole week. If swim and gym must happen close together on Tue or Fri, keep the gym to the RPE caps and drop the final accessory first.

Your two gym sessions are on Tuesday and Friday; every other day in this block is one of your triathlon sessions, taken from the schedule you gave, so the week covers more days than two without adding gym work.
