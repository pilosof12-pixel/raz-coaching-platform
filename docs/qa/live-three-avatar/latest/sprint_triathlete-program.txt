This is Weeks -8 to -5 before race day: a prepeak-specificity build block, not the taper. The goal here is to keep the key triathlon work specific and realistic from current capacity, give the swim two direct touches each week because it is the cheapest limiter to improve, and hold squat, single-leg, trunk and calf strength at a recoverable dose.

Weeks 2-4: Monday run quality progresses one variable at a time, Tuesday’s main swim set is the progression-bearing swim anchor each week, the second weekly swim stays direct and event-relevant, and Week 4 cuts non-essential volume so you finish the block fresher without losing useful intensity.

If right achilles stiffness shows up during Monday intervals or the next morning, repeat the last symptom-free Monday first before cutting Wednesday or Saturday. If stiffness follows the long run, trim Saturday by 10-15 minutes first. Keep hills and any extra run day out. Keep the heavy slow calf work unchanged unless symptoms appear during the raises themselves.
