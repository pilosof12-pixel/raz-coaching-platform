Weeks -8 to -5 from race day: this is a prepeak-specificity build, not the taper. Monday is the flat run interval anchor near current 5 km pace, Tuesday carries the main swim progression, and Tuesday/Friday strength stays low-cost to hold squat, single-leg, trunk, hip and calf work without stealing from the triathlon work.

Weeks 2-3 move one variable at a time: Monday adds one rep, then nudges pace; Tuesday swim extends the rep length at the same split; strength mostly holds its standards. Week 4 is a consolidation week: volume drops, but run pace feel, swim split, bike power and squat intensity stay honest.

If the right achilles stiffens during Monday intervals or the next morning, repeat the last symptom-free Monday dose rather than adding reps or pace; keep Wednesday and Saturday flat and unchanged. No hills and no extra runs. If Friday lifting makes Saturday feel heavy, remove or reduce Friday Reverse Lunge before touching the calf work.

Your two gym sessions are on Tuesday and Friday; every other day in this block is one of your triathlon sessions, taken from the schedule you gave, so the week covers more days than two without adding gym work.
