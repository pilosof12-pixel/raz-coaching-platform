This block keeps the squat and One-Arm Pull-up work in the highest-value slots, caps extra pressing and accessory volume so MMA stays the priority, and keeps the marathon work to one easy long run.

Weeks 2-3 nudge one main lever at a time: Monday squat load, One-Arm Pull-up execution or assistance quality, and long-run distance. Pressing dose stays steady and subordinate. Week 4 trims fatigue, shortens the long run, and keeps the best Week 3 standard without forcing extra work.

If elbows or biceps tendon get cranky, make Monday One-Arm Pull-up all singles and add a touch more help on Tuesday. If squat readiness is poor, hold the last clean load and cut a set before forcing the jump. If the long run leaves your legs flat for lifting, shorten it by 10-20% and keep the pace truly easy.
