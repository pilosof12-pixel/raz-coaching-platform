Week 1 puts the best energy into your two primaries: strict One-Arm Pull-up quality and the heavy Back Squat exposure. OHP gets one direct strict slot plus one lower-cost overload slot, and marathon work stays to one real long run so MMA quality and recovery stay protected.

No injury limits were reported. If elbows or forearms feel beat up from One-Arm Pull-up or grappling, keep Monday to clean singles after the first strict set, add a little more help on Assisted One-Arm Pull-up, and hold Friday Weighted Chin-up to one crisp set. If hips, knees, or low back feel overly taxed from MMA, keep Friday Back Squat to one clean set and cut accessories before touching Monday’s primary work.

Your four gym sessions are on Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Sunday; every other day in this block is one of your mma sessions, taken from the schedule you gave, so the week covers more days than four without adding gym work.
