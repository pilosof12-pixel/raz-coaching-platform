Built to keep the primary Back Squat and One-Arm Pull-up work protected, keep a real strict Overhead Press progression, and preserve one direct weekly run without letting the marathon side quest steal recovery from MMA.

Weeks 2-4: squat and One-Arm Pull-up progress mainly by small load/rep/assistance changes if Week 1 stays clean; the long run progresses by a small distance increase in Weeks 2-3, then consolidates in Week 4. Fri stays intentionally low-cost and is the first day to trim if MMA fatigue spikes.

If elbows or forearms get irritated, keep the strict One-Arm Pull-up reps very crisp and reduce Assisted One-Arm Pull-up assistance demands before cutting the direct strict work. If knees or low back feel beat up, keep both Back Squat exposures but reduce accessory lower work first. Keep the run easy; if impact soreness rises, hold the prior week’s distance instead of forcing the increase.
