The long run stays easy and conversational so squat, One-Arm Pull-up work, and MMA keep the recovery priority.

Weeks 2-3 only nudge one main variable at a time: Monday squat load, One-Arm Pull-up quality or assistance, and the long run from 16 km to 18 km to 20 km if recovery stays good. Week 4 trims accessory, back-off, and long-run volume while keeping moderate intensity so you finish fresher without losing the strength and skill you built.

If elbows or the biceps tendon get cranky, Monday One-Arm Pull-up drops to 1 + 1 + 1 per side and Tuesday assistance increases instead of forcing strict reps. If sparring leaves hips or adductors tight and squat depth slows, repeat the last clean squat load and cut the final squat set. If shins, feet, or Achilles get irritated on the long run, stop the progression there and repeat the last comfortable distance instead of forcing pace.

You asked for four sessions a week, and that is what this is: four formal strength sessions. They sit across five calendar days because the easy running and the mobility work are not sessions in that sense -- they are the low-cost work around them, and they are not what you count when you count your week.
