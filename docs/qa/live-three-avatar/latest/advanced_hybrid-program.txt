Priority stays on squat and One-Arm Pull-up quality while keeping a real strict-press progression and one recoverable weekly long run. Friday is intentionally low-cost because your gym + MMA already fills all 7 days.

Weeks 2-4: only the primary levers move. Squat load edges up if Week 1 bar speed/RPE allow, strict One-Arm Pull-up set count stays stable while rep quality/assistance improve, pressing stays submaximal, and the long run progresses by a small distance increase only. Week 4 keeps the best Week 3 standards with slightly less fatigue.

Pain/substitutions: if elbows or biceps tendon feel cranky, keep strict One-Arm Pull-up crisp and cut Assisted One-Arm Pull-up volume before touching the strict singles. If shoulders are beat up from MMA, keep Overhead Press at the low end of the load range and remove the final row on Fri first. If a knee/hip flare shows up, reduce Back Squat load 2.5-5% and keep depth/tempo clean rather than forcing the calendar jump.
