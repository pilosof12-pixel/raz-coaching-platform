Primary work stays compact and high-specificity: Monday drives strict One-Arm Pull-up plus the heavy squat, Tuesday keeps the assisted unilateral pull clean and low-cost, Friday is deliberately recovery-contingent, and Sunday pushes the strict press without stealing from Monday.

Weeks 2-3 nudge the heavy squat and Sunday press load up, reduce assistance on Tuesday One-Arm Pull-up, and extend the long aerobic run by 1 km at the same easy effort. Week 4 is a consolidation week: fewer support sets, moderate intensity kept on the main lifts, and the best Week-3 skill standard is retained without chasing fatigue.

If elbows or forearms flare on the pulling work, keep Monday One-Arm Pull-up to clean singles, add assistance back on Tuesday, and drop Friday Chin-up first. If squat or press pain appears, repeat the last clean load or cut one set; if Saturday legs are still flat from Friday sport, hold the previous week’s long-run distance instead of forcing the next step.

On Marathon: this block holds it at a maintenance dose rather than developing it, on purpose. 220kg back squat and 100kg overhead press are what this block develops, and 5 sport sessions a week already own most of the recovery budget. Keeping the exposure protects what you already have and keeps it ready to progress in the next block; it is not an attempt to improve it in these four weeks.
