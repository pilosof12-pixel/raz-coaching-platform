Weeks 2-4: Week 2 makes small recoverable steps, Week 3 is the hardest quality week with the heaviest squat/OHP loads and the longest long run, and Week 4 consolidates by trimming non-essential volume and shortening the run so you finish fresher, not flat.

If elbows or the biceps tendon get cranky, make Monday One-Arm Pull-up all singles and add a measurable bit of assistance on Tuesday. If squat depth, bracing, or bar speed slips, hold the last successful load and cut the last back-off first. If feet, shins, or calves get beaten up from MMA plus the run, keep the run fully conversational and match the planned distance at easy effort rather than forcing pace.

On Marathon: this block holds it at a maintenance dose rather than developing it, on purpose. 220kg back squat and 100kg overhead press are what this block develops, and 5 sport sessions a week already own most of the recovery budget. Keeping the exposure protects what you already have and keeps it ready to progress in the next block; it is not an attempt to improve it in these four weeks.

You asked for four sessions a week, and that is what this is: four formal strength sessions. They sit across five calendar days because the easy running and the mobility work are not sessions in that sense -- they are the low-cost work around them, and they are not what you count when you count your week.
