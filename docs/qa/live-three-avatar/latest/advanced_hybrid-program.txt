Here’s your compact 4-week block: squat and strict One-Arm Pull-up stay first, strict Overhead Press stays in, and the single weekly long run remains the marathon anchor without stealing recovery from MMA.

Weeks 2-3 progress the heavy squat top set, One-Arm Pull-up execution/assistance, and the long run distance by small steps. Strict press is kept meaningful but mostly steady to protect recovery. Week 4 is a consolidation week: similar main-lift intensity, less non-essential volume, finish fresher.

If elbow or biceps-tendon stress shows up, keep Monday One-Arm Pull-up to singles only and use one more step of assistance on Tuesday. If squat or press pain, or bar speed falls off early, repeat the last clean load and cut one back-off or accessory set. If the long run hurts Wednesday MMA quality, repeat the last successful distance instead of forcing the next step.

On 100kg overhead press: this block holds it at a maintenance dose rather than developing it, on purpose. 220kg back squat and Marathon are what this block develops, and 5 sport sessions a week already own most of the recovery budget. Keeping the exposure protects what you already have and keeps it ready to progress in the next block; it is not an attempt to improve it in these four weeks.
