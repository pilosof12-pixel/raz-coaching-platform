Built to protect your squat and One-Arm Pull-up progress first, keep a real but recoverable OHP pathway, and preserve one direct weekly run without letting the marathon side quest steal from MMA or lower-body recovery. Friday stays intentionally low-cost because it lands on a hard MMA day.

Weeks 2-4: only the main priorities progress meaningfully if bar speed, technique, elbows, and overall recovery stay on track. Squat progresses through small load bumps, One-Arm Pull-up through more clean singles or slightly less assistance, OHP through repeatable submax work, and the run through a small long-run extension before consolidating.

If elbows, biceps tendon, or medial forearm get irritated, first trim Chin-up load or one set before touching the direct One-Arm Pull-up rows. If shoulders feel beat up from MMA, keep Overhead Press crisp and submaximal and skip the final optional accessory on Friday. If knee or hip fatigue is high after MMA, keep Friday Back Squat at the low end of the load range.
