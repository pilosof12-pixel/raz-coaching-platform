Match day is Wednesday and Saturday, and every session in this block is placed by its distance from match day. Two short gym touches this week: early tendon isometrics, crisp box jumps, one direct trap-bar strength touch, and minimum-effective upper-body maintenance. Court work already gives you the repeat-effort conditioning, so the gym dose stays low-cost and is there to keep you available and keep your jump output from getting blunted by fatigue.

Back squat is out this block because heavy slow squatting inside the fixture cycle is the thing that flares your tendon. If knee pain during Wall Sit or split-squat work rises above your normal game-week level, shorten the hold or ROM and keep the effort submaximal; if that still feels wrong, swap that assistance slot for Glute Bridge. On Trap Bar Deadlift and Bench Press, if the RPE cap is reached early, hold the load or stop one set short rather than forcing the listed volume.
