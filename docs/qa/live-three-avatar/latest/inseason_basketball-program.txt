Match day is Wednesday and Saturday, and every session in this block is placed by its distance from match day. No added conditioning here: practice and games already cover that. This block keeps the gym dose short, direct and repeatable so you stay available, keep some jump pop in the week, and hold lower-body strength without poking the tendon. Back squat is deliberately out for now; Trap Bar Deadlift plus split-squat work covers the same job with a friendlier symptom profile in this schedule.

Weeks 2-4: Week 2 adds one small Monday jump set if height stays crisp. Week 3 keeps the jump dose steady and lengthens the tendon isometric slightly if the knee response stayed normal. Week 4 cuts non-essential volume and trims a rep from the main lifts so you finish fresher, not flatter.

Pain guidance: keep tendon pain during the session at or below your usual training level, and if the next morning is clearly worse than your normal post-game baseline, first reduce split-squat load or ROM, then repeat the last clean week. Thursday is the recovery-contingent day after Wednesday’s game; if you come in heavy, keep the quality work and drop the last accessory first.
