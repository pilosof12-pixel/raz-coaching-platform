You’re in late camp rolling into fight week, so this is a taper-maintenance block: brief gym touches to keep force, snap, and confidence in familiar patterns while fatigue falls. Every set should feel sharp and leave you fresher than a normal strength phase would.

Week 2 only nudges the Tuesday Pull-up load if Week 1 stayed crisp under the cap; otherwise repeat it. Week 3 cuts sets across both days while keeping speed and familiar patterns. Week 4 is fight week: Tuesday is the last meaningful touch, and Friday is a very short Day -3 primer.

Right knee stays out of heavy Back Squat. If knee ache rises above your usual baseline or next-day stiffness is clearly worse, keep Barbell Hip Thrust and Prowler Push at the low end, cut the last planned lower-body set first, and if needed swap that day’s lower-body slot to the other tolerated option rather than forcing through it.
