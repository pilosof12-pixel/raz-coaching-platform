This block starts 3.4 weeks out and runs straight into fight week, so it is a late-camp hold/taper block, not a build. The gym work is there to keep strength feel, speed and confidence without stealing recovery from MMA or the cut. Hard sparring should come down across the block: about 2 hard contacts in Week 1, 1 in Week 3, and none in fight week beyond technical/tactical speed work.

Week 2 is mostly a repeat, with only a small Pull-up load nudge if Week 1 stayed crisp. Week 3 cuts volume and keeps speed. Week 4 is a Day -4 touch plus an optional Day -1 neural primer only.

No Back Squat this block. If the right knee ache goes above your usual level or next-day stiffness is worse than normal, keep the throws, Pull-up and Hip Thrust, and drop Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat or Prowler Push first. In fight week, if sleep is poor, the cut is biting, or you already feel sharp, skip the Day -1 primer entirely. Weight-cut details themselves are medical/team calls, not something to solve by adding training.
