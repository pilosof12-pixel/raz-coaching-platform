This is a late-camp taper block: two short full-body gym touches each week to keep force and speed without stealing from MMA. Week 4 is fight week, so the only gym work is brief primer work placed on Day -5 and Day -2 to keep you sharp and fresh.

Weeks 2-3 keep the same familiar movements and mostly the same load feel while trimming sets first. Week 4 is primer work only: fast singles or short sets, full rest, and stop while everything still feels snappy.

No heavy bilateral squatting in this block. If the right-knee ache climbs above your normal mild baseline or next-day stiffness lingers, shorten the Prowler Push, keep Barbell Hip Thrust to one set, and strip the final accessory first; weight-cut details are medical and outside this program.
