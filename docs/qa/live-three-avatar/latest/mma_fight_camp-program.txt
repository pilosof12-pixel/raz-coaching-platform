These are short gym touches built around camp, not extra training to chase gains. The slightly heavier strength signal sits on Tuesday; Friday stays deliberately low-cost so you keep the strength/power feel without stealing from MMA or the cut.

Week 2 only nudges the Tuesday weighted Pull-up if Week 1 stayed crisp. Week 3 trims total work while keeping speed and familiar loading, and Week 4 is just a primer, finish each session feeling better than when you started.

Right knee rule: keep all lower-body work pain-free during the set and no worse than your normal next-day stiffness. If the knee or overall fatigue is up, keep the Prowler lighter, cap the main lifts at the first crisp set, and drop the final accessory before forcing volume.
