This block runs into the event, now about 3.1 weeks away: the final week is competition week, not another training week. This is the 4-week arrival block into the fight: 2 late-camp hold weeks, 1 taper week, then fight week. The gym work stays minimum-effective-dose only, fast throws, a few heavy familiar strength touches, and no extra conditioning, so you arrive sharp, not tired. From here, bring hard sparring down; the last truly damaging hard round should sit early in Week 3, with no hard contact in fight week beyond fast technical work.

Weeks 2-4: Week 2 intentionally repeats Week 1 rather than chasing PRs if speed and recovery stay good. Week 3 trims volume again, and Week 4 becomes a short Tuesday touch plus an optional Friday primer after weigh-in; the smaller numbers are the taper doing its job, not lost training.

Knee rule: no Back Squat in this block. If the right knee goes above your usual 3/10 or next-day stiffness is worse than baseline, keep load the same, shorten Reverse Lunge depth slightly, and swap that row for Barbell Hip Thrust if needed. If any main lift overshoots the RPE cap or loses speed, cut the last set and hold last week’s load next time. Weight-cut method itself stays with your fight coach/medical team; this plan just reduces training cost around it.
