This block runs into the event, now about 4 weeks away: the final week is competition week, not another training week. This is your 4-week arrival block into Hyrox: Week 1 and 2 realize race pace and station rhythm, Week 3 tapers, and Week 4 is competition week with Day 0 on Monday. The main target is holding Hyrox pace through transitions while your squat and pulling stay on minimum effective maintenance. Where a note names SkiErg, sled pull, wall ball, or burpee broad jump, use that exact Hyrox station setup.

Week 2 extends continuity and race order rather than chasing faster paces. Week 3 keeps race pace and race loads but trims total work. Week 4 is for freshness: same four touchpoints, much less volume, crisp singles, and the Day -4 primer is optional. After this block, the two weeks to the half should be recovery-first, then one easy long-run rebuild and one light pace touch only if the Achilles stays quiet.

If the left Achilles grumbles, change the next run exposure first: cut the last rep or shorten the easy run, and if stride changes replace that day’s Run with Rowing Ergometer for matched work time. Do not add a make-up run on the next day. Keep calf work smooth, and stop any run rep that changes your mechanics.
