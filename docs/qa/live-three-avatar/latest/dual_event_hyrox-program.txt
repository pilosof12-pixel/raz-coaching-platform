These 4 weeks are Weeks -4 to -1 into Hyrox, so this is a realization-to-taper block aimed at peaking the A race, not both races at once. Hyrox gets the sharpest work, your squat and pulling stay on maintenance, and the running stays direct but trimmed as race week approaches so you arrive fresh.

Week 2 gives you one continuous race-order rehearsal. Week 3 cuts specific volume but keeps the race-feel station-to-run touch while stripping out extra work. Week 4 is competition week: same training days, much less work, and the Day -3 primer is optional. After Hyrox, the next two weeks should be recovery first, then one modest long easy run plus one light half-marathon pace touch; do not try to rebuild volume immediately.

If the left Achilles grumbles, change the run that caused it: replace the next run row with easy Rowing Ergometer or Rower for the same time and effort, keep the calf work in, and cut the fast running reps before you cut squat or pulling. If pain rises during a station-to-run pair, stop at the last clean rep and hold that week’s dose.
