This is your final 4-week Hyrox runway into Day 0: Weeks 1-2 sharpen race execution, Week 3 tapers volume while keeping race pace and race load in touch, and Week 4 is competition week. Hyrox is the A race here; the half marathon two weeks later is protected by keeping one direct easy run pathway alive without spending freshness that belongs to race day.

Weeks 2-4 progress one lever at a time: Week 2 extends the specific work and the rehearsal, Week 3 trims volume but keeps race feel, and Week 4 becomes short, familiar sharpness only. The two weeks after this block should be recovery-first after Hyrox, then one easy reload run and one controlled longer easy run before any harder pace work returns.

Achilles rule: if the left Achilles grumbles, change the run stressor most likely responsible first. Shorten the next run rep or cut the Saturday run by 10-15 minutes; if needed, swap that run for matched easy Rowing Ergometer time. Keep squat and non-impact stations unless they also provoke, and trim Wall Ball or Burpee Broad Jump contacts before touching the key Monday/Wednesday race-order work.

You asked for four sessions a week, and that is what this is: four formal strength sessions. They sit across five calendar days because the easy running and the mobility work are not sessions in that sense -- they are the low-cost work around them, and they are not what you count when you count your week.
