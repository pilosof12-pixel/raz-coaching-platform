This is your 4-week realization-to-race block, starting 3.9 weeks from Hyrox. Tuesday is the main Hyrox quality day, Friday keeps your second direct run for half-marathon durability, and squat plus pulling strength stay on a minimum-effective dose so freshness rises into Day 0.

Week 2 turns Tuesday into the race-order rehearsal and extends Friday’s easy run from 45 to 55 minutes. Ski pace stays near 1:49-1:51 per 500 m while the reps get more race-like, then Week 3 cuts volume without losing pace or race-load feel, and Week 4 is competition week: short readiness touches only. The two weeks after Hyrox should be recovery-first, then one rebuilt long easy run and one short HM-pace touch if the Achilles stays quiet.

If the left Achilles grumbles, change the next run stressor first: swap the next fast or transition Run for Bike or Rower at the same total time and effort, keep Burpee Broad Jump and Wall Ball to the low end, and only return to the planned run after a day off. In Week 4, any pace miss, bar-speed drop, or rising soreness means stop there.
