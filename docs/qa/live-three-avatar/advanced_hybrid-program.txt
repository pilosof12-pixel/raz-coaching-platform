Here is your four-week program designed to balance your primary strength and calisthenics goals with your MMA training and marathon side quest. This plan prioritizes your 220kg Back Squat, 4 One-Arm Pull-ups, and 100kg Overhead Press, while building a sustainable foundation for your marathon.

We'll use your current strength benchmarks to guide your working loads, focusing on quality and submaximal efforts to manage recovery alongside your demanding MMA schedule. Your Friday gym session is intentionally lighter to accommodate your hard MMA day. The marathon progression will be gradual, building weekly distance at an easy Zone 2 pace.

**Progression Notes for Weeks 2-4:**

* **Back Squat**: You'll progressively increase the load on your heavy Monday session, aiming to build toward your 220kg goal. On Fridays, we'll gradually increase volume or maintain quality at a lower intensity.
* **One-Arm Pull-up**: For your strict One-Arm Pull-up, we'll focus on maintaining high-quality singles and potentially adding a set. For Assisted One-Arm Pull-up, you'll aim to increase reps or reduce assistance over the weeks.
* **Overhead Press**: We'll progressively increase the load on your direct Overhead Press on Sundays, working towards your 100kg goal. Your Push Press on Mondays will provide a complementary strength stimulus.
* **Marathon Run**: Your weekly long run will gradually increase in distance at an easy Zone 2 pace, building your aerobic base without excessive fatigue. Week 4 will be a consolidation run at a slightly reduced distance.
* **Accessories**: Volume for accessory work will remain conservative and can be adjusted or removed if recovery becomes an issue.
* **Week 4**: This will be a consolidation week, reducing overall volume slightly to manage fatigue while maintaining intensity on your key lifts and skills.

**Pain and Substitution Guidance:**
If you experience any pain during an exercise, stop immediately. Do not push through pain. If a specific exercise consistently causes discomfort, please note it in your results, and we can explore alternative movements or modifications in the next block. Prioritize recovery and listen to your body, especially with your high MMA volume.
