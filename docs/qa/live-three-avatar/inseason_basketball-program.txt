Match day is Wednesday and Saturday, and every session in this block is placed by its distance from match day. Built for the Wednesday/Saturday fixture cycle: court work already covers your repeat-effort conditioning, so the gym stays short and low-fatigue. Monday keeps the direct trap-bar strength anchor plus upper-body maintenance; Thursday is the deliberately low-cost knee-dominant and tendon-support day so you keep showing up live.

Weeks 2-4: Week 2 nudges jump quality with a slightly higher box only if Week 1 was clean. Week 3 holds that jump dose and only nudges the trap bar if Week 2 stayed under the cap. Week 4 is consolidation: same moderate intensity, less total work, so finishing fresher is the win.

Patellar tendon rule: if pain rises above 3/10 during the session or the next morning is clearly worse than your usual game-week baseline, keep box height and trap-bar load at the last clean week, cut the split-squat work before touching the upper-body rows or bench, and only keep the Belt Squat holds if symptoms are settling by the second hold.
