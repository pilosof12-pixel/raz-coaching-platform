Here is your personalized 4-week program designed to help you achieve your first bar muscle-up and a freestanding handstand, while building a strong general push and pull foundation. We'll focus on high-quality skill practice and controlled progression, respecting your 13-year-old body and your available equipment.

The program emphasizes skill work while fresh, followed by support strength. For your bar muscle-up, we'll work on explosive pulling and specific transition drills. For the handstand, we'll combine wall holds for body line and endurance with controlled kick-ups for balance practice. Since you have no external weights, we'll use tempo, pauses, and rep progression for strength, and bands for assistance where needed.

Over the next four weeks, you'll see a clear progression in your skill work. For the Bar Muscle-up Transition Drill, we'll aim to reduce band assistance or improve the smoothness of the turnover. For Controlled Handstand Kick-ups, the goal is to increase successful attempts and brief moments of independent balance. Your support strength exercises will progress by adding reps, sets, or increasing the difficulty of the variation. Week 4 will consolidate your best performance from Weeks 2 and 3 with slightly reduced volume to manage fatigue, ensuring you retain the quality you've built.

Remember to prioritize perfect form over extra reps, especially for skill work. If you experience any pain, stop the exercise and consider a temporary substitution or modification. Listen to your body and adjust as needed.
