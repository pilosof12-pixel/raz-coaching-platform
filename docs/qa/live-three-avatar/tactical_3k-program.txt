Here is your four-week program designed to improve your 3 km run, 10 km ruck, and strict pull-ups, while maintaining your strength and athleticism. The program integrates your current running and rucking schedule with three strength sessions per week, carefully managing impact due to your shin-splint history.

We've adjusted the weekly schedule to fit within your five available training days, spacing out your strength sessions and combining compatible endurance work on strength days to optimize recovery and progression. This structure also helps distribute impact from running and rucking to respect your shin-splint history.

**Progression Notes for Weeks 2-4:**

* **Strength Lifts (Squat, Deadlift, Overhead Press):** Aim to increase the load by 2.5-5 kg when you successfully complete all sets and reps at the target RPE with good form. If you don't hit the top of the rep range, stay at the same load and try to hit more reps or improve form.
* **Weighted Pull-up:** Maintain the +30 kg x 5 for strength. If you consistently hit this at RPE 8, you may attempt +32.5 kg for 4-5 reps in later weeks.
* **Strict Pull-up:** Gradually increase the number of reps per set or add an extra set as your endurance improves, while maintaining quality.
* **Running Intervals:** We will progress by either increasing the total interval volume (e.g., adding more 400m repeats or slightly increasing the duration of the work) or by slightly increasing the pace. Week 4 will focus on expressing speed with a slight reduction in overall interval volume to ensure freshness.
* **Easy & Aerobic Runs:** Gradually increase the duration or distance of these runs to build your aerobic base.
* **Ruck:** We will maintain the 20 kg load for 10 km and gradually improve your pace across the weeks, starting with a pace slightly faster than your current benchmark.
* **Shin-Splint Management:** Pay close attention to your body. If you experience any shin irritation, reduce the volume or intensity of your running or rucking for a session or two, then gradually reintroduce the progression. Consistency at a manageable load is key.
