Here is your four-week program, designed to improve your 3 km run, 10 km ruck, and strict pull-ups while maintaining your strength and athleticism.

The program is structured across five training days to effectively balance your running, rucking, and strength goals while managing impact and recovery, especially given your shin-splint history. We'll use your current performance markers to build a progressive plan.

For your running, we'll start with intervals based on your recent 400m pace and gradually progress either the pace or the total work, but not both simultaneously, to manage shin impact. Your ruck will progress similarly, focusing on maintaining the 20 kg load while improving pace or distance. Pull-up progression will involve increasing reps or adding load where appropriate.

Remember to prioritize recovery, especially sleep and nutrition. If you experience any shin irritation or other pain, reduce the intensity or volume of the provocative activity and focus on pain-free movement. You can substitute a run with a lower-impact aerobic activity like cycling or rowing if needed, but aim to return to running as soon as symptoms allow.

---
### Weeks 2-4 Progression:

**Running:** We'll gradually increase the total work or slightly improve the pace of your interval sessions, ensuring your shins tolerate the load. Easy runs will maintain aerobic base. Week 4 will consolidate and allow for a slightly faster expression of speed on intervals.
**Rucking:** We'll aim to improve your pace or slightly extend the distance of your 20 kg ruck, carefully monitoring your recovery and shin response.
**Strength:** For Back Squat, Deadlift, and Overhead Press, you'll aim to hit the top of the rep range at the target RPE, then increase the load slightly in subsequent weeks. Weighted Pull-ups will progress by adding reps or a small amount of load. Strict Pull-ups will focus on increasing quality reps per set. Support work will remain consistent to provide a stable base.
