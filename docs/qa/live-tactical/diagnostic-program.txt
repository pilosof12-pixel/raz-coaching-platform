Hello! This four-week block is designed to build your 3 km running and 10 km ruck performance, increase your strict pull-up capacity, and maintain your key strength lifts. We'll be carefully progressing your running and rucking volume and intensity, while managing impact to respect your shin-splint history. Your strength sessions will support these goals without adding unnecessary fatigue.

Progression Notes:
Across the next four weeks, your running intervals will gradually increase in volume or speed, while your easy and longer aerobic runs will build duration. Rucking will progress by increasing distance or slightly improving pace. Your strict pull-up volume will increase, and weighted pull-ups will follow a double progression model. Your main strength lifts will progress load when the top of the rep range is hit, or consolidate strength for maintenance.

Pain and Substitution Guidance:
Listen closely to your body, especially your shins. If any shin pain or irritation arises, immediately reduce the volume or intensity of your running or rucking sessions. If symptoms persist, substitute high-impact running with lower-impact aerobic options like cycling or rowing, but aim to maintain your specific running and rucking frequency at a reduced, pain-free dose. For strength work, if a movement causes pain, stop and substitute with a pain-free variation or remove it for the session.
