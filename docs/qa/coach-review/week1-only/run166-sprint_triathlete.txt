This block sits about 7.9 to 4.9 weeks from race day, so it is a prepeak-specific build rather than a taper. The emphasis stays on the cheapest limiter to improve first, your swim, while keeping bike work specific and preserving your current three non-consecutive run exposures.

Weeks 2 and 3 nudge the main swim, bike, and run work up one step at a time while the strength work mostly holds its job. Week 4 is a consolidation week: less nonessential volume, same general intensity, and a fresher finish without going flat.

If the right Achilles is sore during a run or is stiffer the next morning than your normal baseline, first reduce the run session that most likely caused it: Monday interval reps or the Saturday pick-up segment. Repeat the last clean week before cutting the Wednesday easy run or the stable easy portion on Saturday, and do not add hills or any extra run day. On Tuesday strength, skip the second split-squat set if Monday still left the calves or tendon loaded.

Your two gym sessions are on Tuesday and Friday; every other day in this block is one of your triathlon sessions, taken from the schedule you gave, so the week covers more days than two without adding gym work.
