Week 1 sits 7.9 weeks out from race day, so this is a prepeak-specific build week, not a taper. The bias is toward the swim because it is your biggest and cheapest limiter to improve, while the bike is held with enough race-relevant work and the run stays on the same three non-consecutive days your achilles already tolerates. The two Tue/Fri gym sessions stay low-fatigue and full-body so you keep squat, single-leg strength, calf capacity, trunk and hip work without stealing from the triathlon sessions. This week uses a controlled bike-to-run brick rather than forcing a bigger rehearsal, because protecting the no-consecutive-run rule matters more than cramming in extra transition load.

If the right achilles stiffens during training or the next morning, adjust the provoking run first, not the whole week. First change Monday by cutting one interval or easing the reps to 4:40-4:45/km, or shorten Saturday's off-bike run to 4 km at the same pace. Keep Wednesday flat and easy, skip hills entirely, do not add any extra running day, and keep the slow calf work unless that is the specific irritant. On squat/lunge work, reduce load or range before skipping the session.

Your two gym sessions are on Tuesday and Friday; every other day in this block is one of your triathlon sessions, taken from the schedule you gave, so the week covers more days than two without adding gym work.
