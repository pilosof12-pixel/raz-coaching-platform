This keeps five training days with three formal strength sessions on Tue, Wed and Fri. Tuesday stays the key 3K session, Monday keeps the direct ruck, and Saturday is only easy aerobic running. Strength stays useful and recoverable so the run quality leads.

Weeks 2-3 lengthen the Tuesday reps while holding the pace band steady so only one main running stressor moves at a time; Week 3 also reaches the full 10 km ruck at the same 20 kg. Week 4 is a consolidation week: lower accessory and bodyweight volume, lower total interval volume, and a small speed expression on Tuesday so you finish fresher rather than flat.

If shin irritation shows up during or within 24 hours of Tuesday’s intervals, reduce that session first: stop one rep early or repeat the last symptom-free pace/distance next week. If symptoms show up on or after the ruck, hold or slow the ruck pace before changing running. Keep the easy runs conversational, and keep the ruck as brisk walking only, not loaded jogging.

Weekly running volume anchor: your established, tolerated baseline is about 18-20 km per week across 3 running exposures, and this block keeps 3 running exposures while quality becomes more 3K-specific. Counting easy runs, warm-up, repetitions and cooldown, Week 1 projects roughly 15.9 km, which is deliberately below that baseline while the new interval specificity is the only impact variable stepping up. While shins and next-day soreness stay normal, rebuild toward the top of your 18-20 km range by adding a few minutes to the easy runs first, never by adding a fourth running day or by making the easy runs faster. If shin symptoms return, reduce the stressor that provoked them or the one most recently increased, the interval session if symptoms follow that session, the ruck if they follow loaded carrying, and easy-run volume only for diffuse accumulated soreness, then repeat the last symptom-free week before progressing again.

On Improve strict pull-ups from 14 toward 18-20: this block holds it at a maintenance dose rather than developing it, on purpose. Improve 3 km from 13:30 to sub-12:00 and Improve 10 km ruck with 20 kg from 95 min toward 82 min are what this block develops. Keeping the exposure protects what you already have and keeps it ready to progress in the next block; it is not an attempt to improve it in these four weeks.
