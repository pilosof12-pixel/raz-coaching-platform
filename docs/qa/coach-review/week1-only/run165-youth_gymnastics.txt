Two skill-first sessions. Leave at least one rest day between Session A and Session B so the bar muscle-up and handstand work stay fresh.

Normal muscular effort is fine. Sharp or local wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee pain, or a visible form change, means stop that set and regress immediately: use more band or bench help on the bar muscle-up work, lower the kick-up height or stay with Wall Handstand Hold, and swap Ring Dip for Push-up if the shoulder feels pinchy.
