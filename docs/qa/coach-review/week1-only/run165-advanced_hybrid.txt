Here’s Week 1: squat and strict One-Arm Pull-up get the highest-quality work early in the week, strict press gets its own direct day, Friday stays deliberately low-cost so MMA quality holds, and the marathon work stays to one easy long run at your current recoverable frequency.

If elbows or forearms feel irritated on unilateral pulling, keep Monday to clean singles after the first set and add assistance on Tuesday rather than forcing ugly reps. If shoulders feel beat up from MMA, keep push press/OHP at the stated RPE cap and repeat the last clean load. If squat bracing or knee/back comfort drops, hold the load and cut the final set before chasing the written ceiling.

Your four gym sessions are on Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Sunday; every other day in this block is one of your mma sessions, taken from the schedule you gave, so the week covers more days than four without adding gym work.
