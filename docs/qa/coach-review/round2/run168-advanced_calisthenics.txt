Week 1 keeps the primaries first: one heavy and one volume Weighted Pull-up exposure, one set-length and one quality-single Strict Muscle-up exposure, and the straight-arm work is capped and separated so it supports progress without spending the elbow budget.

Recovery lever before pain: if sleep/readiness slips, warm-ups feel slow, or the same loads jump in RPE, first trim Sat Weighted Pull-up from 4x4 to 3x4 and keep Mon Weighted Pull-up unchanged. If the old elbow ache follows straight-arm work, reduce rows in this order: Tue Band-Assisted Straddle Planche, Tue Advanced Tuck Planche, Fri Advanced Tuck Planche, Fri Advanced Tuck Front Lever, and only then Sat Weighted Pull-up before Mon Weighted Pull-up. If symptoms instead follow bent-arm pulling, cut Wed Banded Muscle-up first, then Sat Strict Muscle-up, then Sat Weighted Pull-up, and only then Mon Weighted Pull-up. That order is the default, not a rule to ignore the evidence: if one named row clearly reproduces the ache and an earlier row does not, reduce the reproducing row first and leave the asymptomatic one alone.
