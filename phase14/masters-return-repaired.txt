This block is written for someone about 9 months past their injury, at the performance stage of the return, and it progresses on what the tissue tolerates rather than on the calendar. You’re far enough past the injury, cleared, and symptom-free in the leg that this is a performance return, not rehab. The block builds the actual race tool back on purpose: one easy aerobic erg exposure, one short race-pace reintroduction, and low-spinal-fatigue lower-body and trunk work that supports rowing instead of competing with it.

Weeks 2-4: the easy aerobic work is held mostly steady on purpose while the Wednesday erg session progresses first by a slightly tighter split band, then by longer work reps. Week 4 trims gym volume, keeps moderate loading on the key strength work, and adds a short extra easy erg touch so you finish fresher and a bit more rowing-specific, not detrained.

If back stiffness settles in the warm-up, train. If it climbs above 3/10 during rowing, posture starts folding under fatigue, or any leg symptoms return, stop that row there, repeat the last clean week next time, and keep squat/lunge depth to the clean range while favoring Chest-Supported Row or Seated Cable Row over any unsupported hinge-based pulling. Barbell Hip Thrust trains the same pattern at a fraction of the spinal and joint cost, and it is what rebuilds the capacity a deadlift would ask for. Bring it back when you have finished a block like this one symptom-free and can re-test it with your clinician's input, rather than on a fixed date.
