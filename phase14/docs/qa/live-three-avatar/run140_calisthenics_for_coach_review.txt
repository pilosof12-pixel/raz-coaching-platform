Monday and Thursday drive the two main pulling goals: heavy Weighted Pull-up plus clean Ring Muscle-up work. Tuesday and Friday keep the inversion and straight-arm skill work in, and Wednesday stays deliberately low-cost so the primary pulling can keep moving.

Weeks 2-3 nudge the main qualities without bloating fatigue: Monday pull-up load climbs, Thursday’s Ring Muscle-up ladder grows from a clean double plus singles to a triple top set, Tuesday Back-to-Wall Handstand Push-Up moves from triples to fours, and Wednesday’s Freestanding Handstand Push-up Negative gets longer from 3 to 5 seconds. Week 4 is consolidation: fewer total sets, similar moderate loads, cleaner reps, fresher elbows.

If the left medial elbow rises above its usual short-lived baseline, remove straight-arm stress in this order: Band-Assisted Straddle Planche, then Advanced Tuck Planche, then Advanced Tuck Front Lever. Keep Ring Muscle-up and Weighted Pull-up unless pain also appears during bent-arm pulling; if it does, stop that row, cut the next pull session by 2.5-5 kg or one set, and repeat the last clean week.
