These two post-football lifts are deliberately small and repeatable around a Saturday match. MD-4 holds the squat, sprint exposure, and the only meaningful eccentric hamstring work; MD-2 stays short and sharp with a small repeat-sprint top-up and low-soreness support. In-season, holding your strength and speed on the same weekly schedule is a win.

Weeks 2-3 only nudge one small lever at a time: Tuesday reaches 24 m from Week 2 and then holds, while Week 3 adds one repeat sprint on Thursday and one extra Nordic rep only if the next-day hamstring feels normal. Week 4 is a consolidation week: the main squat load stays in, but the non-essential sets drop so you finish fresher for the next run of matches.

If the right hamstring shifts from mild awareness to grabbing, sharpness, or clear speed loss, stop the running row immediately. If squat pattern or hamstring tightness changes enough to alter position, keep the day submaximal or swap to Box Squat and Machine Hamstring Curl at RPE 6-7; if symptoms linger into the next day or show up outside training, get it checked.
