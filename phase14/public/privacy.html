RAZ Performance Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, how RAZ Performance handles information used to generate and improve your training program. It is a product privacy notice and is not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

We collect the information you submit in the program intake, such as training goals, current performance, training schedule, sport workload, equipment, recovery information, and any pain or movement limitations you choose to provide. We also store your generated program, program-adjustment requests, timestamps, and a personal program code used to retrieve your program.

Why we use it

We use this information to generate, save, retrieve and adjust your training program, operate the service, protect the service from abuse, troubleshoot failures, and review program outputs for quality assurance.

AI processing

Program inputs needed for generation may be processed by the AI service provider used by RAZ Performance. Only submit information that is reasonably necessary for programming. Do not use the intake as a place to submit unrelated sensitive personal information.

Internal quality review

RAZ Performance may review a limited sample of program inputs and outputs to assess coaching quality, identify bugs, and improve the programming system. Internal review is limited to people who need access for those purposes. Where practical, quality review uses the program code rather than unnecessary identifying details.

Storage and service providers

Training data is stored using third-party infrastructure used to operate the product. Service providers may process information only as needed to provide their technical service to RAZ Performance. RAZ Performance does not sell client training data to advertisers.

Access and correction

If you want to ask what information RAZ Performance holds about you, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern, contact RAZ Performance using the contact method provided with your purchase or access email.

Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while it is reasonably needed to provide program retrieval, adjustments, quality assurance, security and product operations. You may contact RAZ Performance to request deletion, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

Security

RAZ Performance uses access controls and private program codes to limit access. No internet service can promise absolute security, so keep your personal program code private and contact RAZ Performance if you believe it has been exposed.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the product changes. The current version will be published at this page.
