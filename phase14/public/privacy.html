Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026
Short version: We collect the training information you choose to provide so the service can generate and adjust your program. This may include health or injury information. Keep your personal code private. You can permanently delete saved coaching data from inside the program page.

1. Operator and contact

This service is operated by Raz Pilosof under the RAZ AI Coaching Platform. Privacy and support enquiries: pilosof12@gmail.com.

2. Information collected

  • training goals, experience, schedule and availability
  • performance benchmarks, equipment and training location
  • sport participation and schedule
  • injuries, pain, restrictions or other health information you choose to provide
  • program preferences, notes and adjustment requests
  • generated programs and operational records needed to provide the service
  • Program Pass activation, expiry, block-completion and included-adjustment usage

3. Purpose and health information

Information is used to generate, present, save and adjust training programs, manage Program Pass access, troubleshoot failures, protect the service from abuse and improve reliability. Health and injury information is used to modify training, not to diagnose or treat a medical condition.

4. Consent

Before a new program is generated, the app asks for consent to process the health, injury and training information you choose to submit for the purpose of generating and adjusting your program.

5. AI and infrastructure providers

Relevant intake information and current program content may be sent to configured third-party AI providers, including OpenAI and configured fallback providers, and to hosting/database providers needed to operate the service. Cloud providers may process information outside Australia.

6. Personal code and Program Pass

Your personal code functions like an access credential. Anyone who has it may be able to load the saved coaching data, so treat it like a password.

One Program Pass provides one personalised 4-week training block, 56 days of access from activation and up to 6 substantive program adjustments. Language switching does not consume an adjustment. A completely new block requires a new Program Pass.

7. Retention

  • generation job records: up to 7 days
  • program/adjustment history: up to 180 days while operationally needed
  • daily operational usage counters: up to 90 days
  • active intake and current program: during the 56-day Program Pass period plus a short post-expiry grace period, unless deleted earlier

Program Pass entitlement records are kept separately from coaching content so deleting coaching data cannot reset paid access or usage limits.

8. Deletion

The Delete my data control permanently removes the saved intake, current program, adjustment history, operational usage records and related generation jobs for that personal code. Deleting coaching data does not create another Program Pass or reset the block or adjustment allowance.

9. Security

Safeguards include server-side secret storage, database access controls, browser-role restrictions, request limits, security response headers, and deletion and retention controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Aggregate analytics

The coaching app records only aggregate allowlisted funnel-event counts by UTC day. It does not store personal coaching codes, IP addresses, intake text, injury details or generated programs in these analytics records.

11. Access, correction and complaints

You may request access or correction through pilosof12@gmail.com. Privacy complaints can first be raised with the operator and, where applicable, with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

12. Training safety

The service provides training programming, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Seek qualified medical care for severe, worsening, unexplained, neurological or traumatic symptoms.
