Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Short version: the platform collects the training information you choose to provide so it can generate and adjust your program. This may include injury or health information. Your personal code is the key to the saved coaching data, so keep it private. You can delete the saved coaching data from inside the program page.

1. Who operates this service

This service is operated by Raz Pilosof under the AI Coaching Engine / RAZ AI Coaching Platform. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or complaints, use the support contact provided with your purchase or displayed at checkout. A dedicated privacy contact address should be added here before broad public launch.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you submit through the coaching intake and adjustment tools, which may include:

  • training goals, experience, schedule and session availability
  • bodyweight and performance benchmarks
  • equipment and training location
  • sport participation and sport schedule
  • injuries, pain, movement restrictions or other health information you choose to provide
  • program preferences, notes and later adjustment requests
  • generated programs, usage counts and operational job records needed to provide the service
  • Program Pass entitlement information such as activation status, access expiry, whether the initial block has been created, and the number of included adjustments used

The platform does not require you to provide more health detail than is reasonably useful for training modification. Do not submit information about another person unless you are authorised to do so.

3. Why we collect and use it

We use the information primarily to generate, present, save and adjust your training program, manage your Program Pass entitlement and service limits, troubleshoot failed generations, protect the platform from abuse, and improve reliability and safety.

Health and injury information is used to modify training recommendations and exercise selection. The platform is not intended to diagnose, treat or provide medical care.

4. Consent for health information

Before generating a new program, the platform asks you to consent to the collection and processing of the health, injury and training information you choose to submit for the purpose of generating and adjusting your program.

5. AI service providers and other processors

To generate or adjust programs, relevant intake information and the current program may be sent to configured third party AI service providers. The production system may use OpenAI and may use another configured AI provider as a fallback. Hosting and database providers are also used to operate the service.

These providers process information on our behalf under their applicable service terms and privacy arrangements. Because cloud and AI providers may operate infrastructure outside Australia, information may be processed or stored overseas. The exact provider configuration may change as the service is improved, but the information is only shared where reasonably necessary to operate the coaching service.

6. How saved access works

The platform assigns a random personal code to saved coaching data. The code functions like an access credential. Anyone who has the code may be able to load the saved intake and program and request adjustments, so you should treat it like a password and avoid sharing it publicly.

A Program Pass is a separate commercial entitlement. At launch, one Program Pass provides one personalised 4-week training block, 56 days of access from activation, and up to 6 substantive program adjustments. A completely new training block requires a new Program Pass.

7. Retention

Operational records are not intended to be kept forever. Current launch retention targets are:

  • generation job records: up to 7 days
  • program and adjustment history: up to 180 days
  • daily operational usage counters: up to 90 days

The active intake and current program are retained while access to that saved program is required, unless you delete the data earlier. Program Pass entitlement records are kept separately from coaching content so the platform can enforce the purchased access period, one-block limit and included adjustment allowance even if coaching data is deleted.

Transaction, accounting or other legal records may also need to be kept separately for periods required by law.

8. Deleting your coaching data

When your program is loaded, you can use the Delete my data control to permanently remove the saved intake, current program, adjustment history, operational usage records and related generation jobs associated with that personal code. Deletion cannot be undone.

Deleting coaching data does not create another Program Pass or reset the one-block or adjustment allowance. A minimal commercial entitlement record may remain so the platform can honour and enforce the terms of the purchase.

9. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask for inaccurate information to be corrected. Contact the operator through the privacy/support contact shown with your purchase. We may need to verify that you are entitled to access the requested information before releasing it.

10. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to reduce unauthorised access, including server side secret storage, database access controls, request limits, restricted browser access to the database, security response headers, and deletion and retention controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Analytics and marketing

Product analytics may be used to understand events such as landing page visits, checkout completion, intake completion, program generation and feature use. Personal coaching codes, full intake text, injury details and generated programs should not be sent to marketing analytics systems.

12. Medical and training safety

The service provides training programming and training modification guidance. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Seek appropriate qualified medical care for severe, worsening, unexplained, neurological or traumatic symptoms, or whenever a health professional is appropriate.

13. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact the operator first through the privacy/support contact supplied with the service so the issue can be investigated. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may have the right to make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the product, payment model, service providers and retention periods change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.
